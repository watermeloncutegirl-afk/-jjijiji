## 5. Hook families (from Scarlett's research)

| Hook family | Example | Viewer intent | Best use | Competition | Conversion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Niche Prompting | "for plumbers use…" | Speak to my situation | Lowest competition | LOWEST | HIGHEST |
| Build/Proof | "I built my CRM in one day" | Show me it's possible | Reach + credibility, feeds services | Medium | HIGHEST |
| Time Collapse | "half a day → 10 min" | Give me my hours back | Attach to every build video | Low | High |
| Subtraction | "3 tools you actually need" | Make the noise stop | **Brand & bio positioning** | Medium | Medium |
| Secret Access | "hidden mode" | Am I using this wrong? | Saves, follows | High | Medium |
| Comparison | "I tested the top 5" | Settle it for me | Evergreen, search | High | Medium |
| Curation | "for X use Y" | Stop deciding for me | Save-bait | HIGHEST | Low |
| Income Proof | "10k/month with this" | Show me the money | High saves, skepticism | Medium | Low (trust) |
| News/Reactive | "X just launched" | Am I falling behind? | Reach only | HIGHEST | Lowest |

**Principles that won in the research:**
- **Result first.** The first frame shows the result, not a face.
- **A finished artifact beats an explanation.** Pitch-deck plugin 77.2K views vs tool stack 5.4K. CRM dashboard 55.5K vs tool list 11.2K.
- **Insider framing raises engagement.**
- **Time is the value unit.** Examples: "invoices in 30s", "website in one afternoon".
- **"For X use Y" is the highest-performing structure.** It removes a decision rather than adding information.

## 6. Instagram rules as of Oct 2026 (algorithm research)

**Hashtags**
- [official] Max **5 hashtags** per post or Reel (since Dec 2025).
- [official] Hashtags don't add reach; they classify the post (Mosseri, Feb 2025).
- Use specific tags. Never #viral, #fyp or #explore.

**Search**
- [official] Instagram search reads the username, name field, bio, captions, hashtags and location.
- [well-supported] The first caption line should be the search phrase, repeated in on-screen text and voiceover.
- [official] Public Reels from professional accounts with 18+ owners have been indexed by Google and Bing since 2025-07-10.

**Ranking**
- [official] The top 3 signals are watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach. Sends matter most for reaching non-followers.
- [official] New Reels go to a small test audience first, then expand if they perform. A 0-follower account relies on this.
- [official] Originality: only the original is recommended. Reposting accounts have been ineligible since 2026-04-30. No watermarks.
- [official] Instagram shows fewer Reels that are muted, blurry, bordered or mostly covered in text.
- [official] Trial Reels need a professional account and roughly 1K followers (threshold unclear). Not available at 0 followers.

**CTAs**
- [official] Instagram has said it won't recommend posts asking people to "comment a specific word" (June 2024). Engagement bait is on Meta's demotion list.
- Use "save this" or "send this to someone who…", or "free template in my community, link in bio". Add comment-keyword or ManyChat CTAs only once there's a follower base.

**Format**
- [official] Reels up to 3 minutes are eligible for recommendation.
- [well-supported] 15–60s is safest for completion.
- [well-supported] Hook in 1–3s, with on-screen text on frame 1.
- [official] 9:16, 1080×1920.
- Safe zone: keep text out of the top ~220px and bottom ~450px.
- [official] Covers are cropped to 3:4 on the grid.
- Use original voiceover or the Instagram music library.
- [well-supported] Post Wed/Thu or weekday evenings.

**New account**
- [official] Switch to a public Creator account.
- [anecdotal] "Warm-up" rituals are vendor myth.
- Don't mass-follow or mass-like.
- [official] There's no shadowban; check Settings › Account Status.

## 10. Data tools available

**Trend APIs**
- IG Graph API Hashtag Search: free, needs a Creator account and a Meta app. Max 30 hashtags per 7 days; no post counts.
- Apify Instagram scrapers: ~$2.30 per 1K results.
- RapidAPI wrappers.
- Google Trends via trendspy or pytrends: free, rate-limited.
- TikTok Creative Center: hashtags by industry.
- Exploding Topics API: $1K+/month; skip.

- `tools/ig_trends/fetch_trends.py` scores hashtags with Google Trends, plus optional IG Graph and Apify data. Keys come from env vars.

**Top tactics across those skills**
1. Keyword-first caption.
2. Repeat the keyword on screen and in the voiceover.
3. 3–5 niche tags.
4. CTAs aimed at sends.
5. One ask per video.
6. No bait wording.
7. A three-layer hook: visual + spoken + 3–7 words of text.
8. Specific numbers beat clever lines.
9. Film 2–3 hook versions and keep the best 3-second hold.
10. Pin a comment with the resource or objection. Original footage only.
