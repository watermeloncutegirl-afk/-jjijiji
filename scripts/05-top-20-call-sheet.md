# Top 20 call sheet: Penryn & Falmouth

These are the 20 best prospects from `leads/penryn.md` and `leads/falmouth.md`, each with a tailored opener and hook.

**Before every call:**
1. Google the business quickly in case a site has gone up recently.
2. Check the number against the TPS/CTPS do-not-call lists.
3. Have the free homepage mockup ready to send (see `02-offer-and-pricing-talk-tracks.md`).

For the full call flow, use `00-master-cold-call-script.md`. Use the matching niche section in `01-niche-scripts.md`, and keep `04-objection-handling.md` open while you call.

**When to call:**
- Cafés: 2:30–4pm, after the lunch rush.
- Barbers and salons: first thing, before 10am, or mid-afternoon.
- Trades: 7:30–8:30am or 5–6pm.

## Tailored openers (fill in [YOUR NAME])

**Barbers on booking apps (Dukes, Vault, Trewcut, Reeves)**
> "Hi, is that [owner]? It's [YOUR NAME]. I'm a web designer here in Falmouth. I'm calling out of the blue, so can I have 30 seconds and you tell me if it's worth chatting? ... I was looking at your Booksy page. You've got hundreds of five-star reviews, which is brilliant. But if someone Googles 'barber Falmouth', they don't really see any of that. I've mocked up a one-page site that pulls your reviews and booking link onto Google. Can I text it over?"

**Busy cafés (Pier Cafe, Koffiji, Good Vibes, Stargazy, Cafe on the Quay)**
> "...I was trying to check your opening hours and found three different answers online. In summer, half your trade is visitors searching 'café near me' on their phones. I've built a quick draft page with your menu, hours and a map, so they all say the same thing. Can I show you? It takes two minutes."

> For Penryn cafés, walking in mid-afternoon with the mockup on your phone usually works better than calling.

**Thai restaurants (Ploy Thai, Thai E San)**
> "...You're clearly doing well, as I saw you book up. At the moment, anyone who wants your menu ends up on a third-party menu site that you don't control. A simple site with your own menu and a booking button would sort that. Want me to send the draft?"

**Trades (Clearwater, England Roofing, Mid-Cornwall Roofing, JB Electrical)**
> "...I'll be quick, as I know you're probably on a job. You've got great reviews on [TrustATrader / your trade body], but people searching 'roofer Penryn' are landing on Checkatrade and getting five quotes. Your own page sends them straight to you. [For the roofers: I noticed you already own your web address, it just doesn't show anything yet.] Can I send you a mockup by text so you can look tonight?"

**Retail (Bolt Vintage, Histed & Roberts)**
> Bolt: "4,000 people follow you on Instagram, but they can't buy anything when you're shut. A small online shop could take orders at 11pm."
> Histed & Roberts: "Most people book eye tests online now. A simple site with a booking form means you don't lose them to the chains."

**Red Chilli**
> "Delivery apps take a big cut of every order. Your own ordering page lets regulars order direct, with no commission." Stick to ordering and avoid anything about reviews or ratings.
