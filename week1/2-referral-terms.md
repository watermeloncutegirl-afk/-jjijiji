# Referral Partner Terms: one-page template

> Copy everything below the line into a Google Doc, replace every [bracket], and share it as "Anyone with the link can view".
> The numbers match the tracker's Settings tab. If you change one, change both.
> This is a practical template, **not legal or tax advice**. Have an accountant look at the tax lines once you're paying real commissions.

---

# [Your business name]: Referral Partner Programme

**Run by:** [Your full name / business name], [UK town/city], United Kingdom · [email]
**Last updated:** [date]

### What you earn
- **15% of the first project**, up to **£750** per client.
- **10% of the client's monthly retainer** (e.g. AI automation support) for their **first 6 paid months**.
- **£150 bonus** when your **3rd** referred client qualifies.
- For clients billed in another currency (e.g. USD), commission is calculated and paid in that currency at the same rates.

### What counts as a referral
1. You submit the client through the **Refer-a-Client form** ([link]) or email an introduction with me in CC. The time of that submission is the referral date.
2. The client **signs and pays at least £500** (or equivalent) within **90 days** of the referral date.
3. The client wasn't already my client, and I wasn't already talking to them. If they were, I'll tell you within 2 business days of your referral.
4. If two people refer the same client, **the first recorded referral wins.**

### When and how you're paid
- Commission is paid **30 days after the client's payment clears**, to cover refunds and chargebacks.
- On projects paid in stages, you're paid your share as each stage clears.
- Retainer commission is paid monthly for months the client has paid.
- If a client is refunded, the matching commission isn't paid (or is deducted from your next payout).
- Payment by bank transfer, PayPal or Wise, once a month. I'll send you a short statement each time.

### Your responsibilities
- **Be upfront.** Tell anyone you recommend me to that you may earn a referral fee. If you recommend me publicly (a post, video or group comment), say so clearly, e.g. "I earn a referral fee if you hire them" or #ad.
- **Get permission** before passing on anyone's contact details.
- **No spam:** no bulk or unsolicited emails, DMs or messages in my name, no paid ads using my name or brand, and no promises about price, timelines or results that I haven't made.
- You're an **independent partner**, not an employee or agent. You can't agree deals or prices on my behalf.

### Tax
- Commission is paid **gross**, with no tax deducted. You're responsible for declaring it and paying any tax or social security due where you live. In the UK, occasional referral income may be covered by the £1,000 trading allowance; check with HMRC or an accountant.
- If you're VAT-registered, send me a VAT invoice for your commission.
- If you're outside the UK, I may ask for your full name and address for my records before the first payout.

### Changes and ending
- Either of us can leave the programme at any time by email.
- I may change or end the programme with **30 days' notice**. Commission already earned (referrals submitted before the change) is still paid under the old terms.
- I can remove a partner who breaks these terms. Unpaid commission from referrals that broke the rules is forfeited.

### Your data
I keep your name, contact and payment details only to run this programme and pay you, and I delete them 12 months after your last payout, unless the law requires me to keep them longer (e.g. accounting records). Ask me any time to see or delete your data.

**By ticking "I accept" on the sign-up form, you agree to these terms.**

## Notes for you (don't paste these into the Doc)

**Why these rules protect you**
- *30-day holdback + refund clause* → you never pay out on money you have to give back.
- *£500 minimum + 90-day window* → stops tiny or stale referrals from costing you.
- *"Already talking to them"* → stops people claiming clients you found yourself. Log every lead in the tracker **with its date** so you have proof.
- *Disclosure line* → UK rules (ASA/CAP Code, CMA) and US rules (FTC) both require paid recommendations to be labelled. The partner is the one posting, but you're responsible for telling them to label it.
- *No-spam clause* → partners' spam could get *your* name reported. You're a UK sender, so PECR/UK GDPR apply to you.

**Your own bookkeeping (UK)**
- Commission you pay is normally an allowable business expense. Keep each payout, the statement and the client invoice it relates to.
- UK businesses don't file a US-style 1099 form. If you ever pay a lot to US-based partners, ask an accountant whether anything is needed.
- Check the numbers against your margin before launch. On a £1,000 site, £150 commission is 15% of *revenue*, which might be 25–30% of your *profit*. If that's too much, lower the rate or raise the minimum deal on the Settings tab.
