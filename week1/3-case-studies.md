# Case Studies: turn your past projects into proof

You have 3+ real projects, so you're ahead of most freelancers. This week you'll turn them into **one template and three formats**, then ask those clients for permission, a testimonial and a referral partnership in one message.

## Step 1: Pick your 3 best
Rank your projects by: **(a) has a number you can quote** (more calls, bookings, sales, hours saved, load time) → **(b) in your chosen niche** → **(c) looks good in a screenshot**.
If a project has no numbers, ask the client (Step 3). Even "we get about 5 more enquiries a week" works.

## Step 2: Fill in this template for each one

```
CLIENT:        [Business name] – [industry], [city/country]
               (or "a [industry] business in [country]" if they'd rather stay anonymous)
PROBLEM:       What was broken or missing? Use their words if you can.
               e.g. "Site from 2016, not mobile-friendly, bookings by phone only, missed calls after 5pm."
WHAT I BUILT:  3–5 bullets. Website + any automation.
               e.g. • 6-page mobile site on [Webflow/WordPress/…]
                    • Online booking synced to their calendar
                    • AI chatbot answering FAQs + capturing leads after hours
TIMELINE:      [x] weeks from kickoff to launch
RESULT:        The number(s). e.g. "Enquiries up from ~8 to ~20/month in the first 2 months."
               No numbers? Use a concrete before/after: "Now takes bookings 24/7 instead of office hours only."
QUOTE:         1–2 sentences from the client (see Step 3).
VISUALS:       Before screenshot · After screenshot (desktop + mobile) · 1 automation screenshot
               (chatbot conversation, booking confirmation, etc.)
LINK:          Live site URL (if the client agrees)
```

> Never make up numbers or quotes. If a result is an estimate, say "about".

## Step 3: Message each past client (permission + testimonial + partner invite)

> Hi [Name], hope [business] is doing well! Quick favour: I'm putting together a few case studies, and [project] is one I'm proudest of.
>
> 1. Would you be OK with me featuring it (name + screenshots)? Happy to keep it anonymous if you prefer.
> 2. Could you give me 1–2 sentences on how it's gone? Any rough numbers help, e.g. more enquiries or time saved.
>
> Also, I've just started a referral programme: if you ever point another business my way and they go ahead, you'd get 15% of their first project (up to £750). Details here if useful: [sign-up form link]. No pressure at all!
>
> Thanks so much, [Your name]

When they sign up, add them on the **Partners** tab with a code (e.g. JANE01). Past clients are your best partners.

## Step 4: Three formats per case study

### A) One-pager (a Google Doc, or a Canva page exported to PDF, to link in DMs)
Headline with the result → Problem → What I built → Result → Quote → 2–3 screenshots → "Want the same for your business? [contact]".
Headline formula: **"How [business type] got [result] with [what you built]"**
e.g. *"How a Manchester plumbing firm doubled enquiries with a new site + 24/7 AI booking"*

### B) LinkedIn / Facebook post (write now, publish in Week 3)
```
[Business type] came to me with [problem in one line].

Here's what we built:
→ [thing 1]
→ [thing 2]
→ [thing 3]

Result after [timeframe]: [number].

"[short client quote]" – [Name, role]

If your [website/booking/lead follow-up] is costing you customers, DM me "AUDIT" and I'll send you a free 3-point review.

[before/after image]
```

### C) Two-line DM version (paste into conversations)
> I did something similar for a [industry] business in [country]: [what you built] → [result]. Here's the 1-page breakdown: [link]

## Step 5: Put the proof where people look
- [ ] **LinkedIn → Featured:** add the 3 one-pagers (or live-site links).
- [ ] **LinkedIn headline:** *"Websites + AI automation for [niche] | [best result, e.g. 'helped a salon take 40% more bookings']"*
- [ ] **Facebook profile intro + featured photos:** before/after screenshots.
- [ ] Save the 3 DM versions somewhere quick to paste (phone notes / text replacement).
