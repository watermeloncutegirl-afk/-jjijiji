# Further study: handling the price question

Optional. These trainers are worth studying, but Vanguard policy (approved numbers, OFF LIMITS, honesty rules) wins wherever a video suggests otherwise. For example, don't adopt any tactic that delays the price past the second ask.

- [3 POWER Moves When Clients Asks, "What's the Price?"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MCSi4tNXytk), Jeremy Miner. Three ways to respond when a prospect asks the price before you have earned the right to quote it.
- [Responding On Price: How To Answer Price Questions on a Cold Call](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t1nojVYjn94), Sabri Suby. What to say when the price question comes up early, so you book the meeting instead of quoting.
- [How I Handle "No Budget" (Cold Call Script)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=59_sEhZojlE), 30 Minutes to President's Club. A script for "we don't have budget" that keeps the conversation alive.
- [Pricing, Price Objections, and Lying Customers](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=siKV9kalIBg), Grant Cardone. Why price objections are often a smokescreen and how to keep control of the conversation.
