# Price rubric and GHL handoff note

## Rubric (REVIEW mode and roleplay debriefs)

Score each item **Pass**, **Partial** or **Miss**, and cite the transcript line.

| # | Item | Pass looks like | Miss looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Answered by the 2nd ask** | Ballpark given no later than the second time they asked | Prospect had to ask a third time, or never got a number |
| 2 | **Approved number only** | "Around two thousand, one-time" / "twenty-six hundred" / "about two hundred a month, cancel anytime" | Any other figure ("a few hundred", "like fifteen hundred"), or "it's cheap" |
| 3 | **Paused after the number** | Said the number, then stopped and let them react | Kept talking, justified or apologised for the price |
| 4 | **Bridged to booking with two options** | "That's what [Closer] works out… Tuesday or Thursday?" | Ended at the price, or asked "When are you free?" (open question) |
| 5 | **Fit qualified** | 2–3 fit questions: decision-maker, taking new customers, how customers find them, budget range | Booked without knowing who decides, or booked a "$300 max" owner |

Also check, as automatic fails if present:

- **OFF LIMITS phrase used**: quote it verbatim.
- **Terms promised**: discounts, payment plans, "special this month", "only two spots left".
- **Wrong timeline**: "14 days from today" instead of "14 days after your kick-off call".
- **Trash talk**: anything negative about Wix, freelancers or the nephew.

**Overall:** 5 Pass with no automatic fails = ready. 3–4 = one focused drill. 2 or fewer, or any automatic fail = repeat the two-ask drill before live calls.

Always finish with: the single moment the call turned, and that moment rewritten with approved wording.

## GHL handoff note template

Log the same day, with the setter as Setter Assigned. Write what the owner actually said; never fill gaps with guesses.

```
SETTER HANDOFF: [Business]
Setter: [Setter name]          Closer: [Closer]
Booked: [Day, date, time, owner's local time]
Link sent: [Text after clear yes / Email] — confirmed received? [Y/N]

DECISION-MAKER
- Spoke with: [Name, role]
- Who decides: [Owner / Owner + spouse / partner]
- Will the decider be on the call? [Y/N]

HOW CUSTOMERS FIND THEM (their words)
- "[quote]"

PRICE / BUDGET SIGNAL
- Asked price? [Y/N]   Ask # when answered: [1/2]
- Ballpark given: [~$2,000 / ~$2,600 / care plan mentioned]
- Their reaction (their words): "[quote]"
- Any number they named: [$ / none]

FIT
- Taking new customers? [Y/N / unclear]
- Current site: [None, Google listing only / existing site: URL]
- Interest in booking / gallery / care plan: [notes]

OBJECTIONS RAISED (their words)
- "[quote]" → setter answer: [approved line used]

UP-FRONT CONTRACT SET? [Y/N]
NOTES FOR CLOSER
- [Anything true and useful: review themes, timing, who answers the phone]
```

If the call did **not** book, log it anyway with the outcome (not a fit – budget, callback time, sent demo by email, do-not-call) and any callback time agreed.
