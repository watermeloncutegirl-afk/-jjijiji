# Compliance and Risk Notes

**Everything here is "check current law."** Rules differ by country and US state and change often. These notes are a checklist of things to confirm, not legal advice. Recommend clients confirm with their own advisor.

## Selling (your outreach)
- DNC: scrub every number against national and internal DNC lists before calling.
- Call 8am–9pm the recipient's local time.
- Text prospects only after a clear verbal yes.
- Cold email: real sender, accurate subject, postal address, working opt-out honored promptly (CAN-SPAM in the US; CASL, GDPR/PECR elsewhere are stricter).
- Don't use AI-generated or prerecorded voices to cold call prospects. Check current FCC/TCPA rules: AI-voice calls have been treated as "artificial or prerecorded voice" calls requiring prior consent.

## What you build for clients
- **Call recording consent.** Some US states require all parties to consent (often called "two-party" or "all-party" states). Have the agent announce recording at the start of the call if recording is on. Check the current state list.
- **AI disclosure.** Have agents identify themselves as automated. Some jurisdictions have or are adding disclosure laws for AI and bots.
- **SMS consent (TCPA).** Missed-call text-back replies to someone who just called; confirm with counsel whether that's covered by the inbound call or needs further consent in the client's situation. Marketing texts generally need prior express written consent. Always honor STOP/opt-out.
- **A2P 10DLC.** US carriers require brand and campaign registration for business texting from 10-digit numbers. Unregistered traffic can be blocked or fined. Registration can take days to weeks; start it at kick-off.
- **Quiet hours** for automated follow-up texts: keep to the client's local daytime hours.
- **Sensitive industries.** Healthcare (HIPAA), legal, finance: don't let agents give advice; escalate to humans; check data-handling requirements before storing transcripts.
- **Reviews.** No incentives for reviews, no review gating (Google policy and FTC rules).
- **Data.** Tell the client where call recordings/transcripts are stored and for how long. Get their approval.

## Business risks to flag in the proposal
- Platform outages (voice or telephony provider) → fallback: calls forward to the owner's phone
- Agent says something wrong → keep a "don't answer" list; review transcripts weekly in month one
- Cost overruns on usage → cap or alert on monthly minutes
