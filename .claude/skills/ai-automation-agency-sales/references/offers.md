# Offer Menu and Fit Signals

Lead with one offer. Add a second only after the first is live and working.

| Offer | What it does | Strong fit signals | Weak fit / skip |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Missed-call text-back** | When a call goes unanswered, sends an SMS from the business number ("Sorry we missed you, how can we help?") and logs the lead | Owner works with their hands (trades, salons, auto); voicemail full or unanswered when you call; reviews mention "hard to reach" | Front desk answers every call; mostly walk-in |
| **AI receptionist / voice agent** | Answers calls (all or after-hours/overflow), answers FAQs from the business's info, books or takes a message, hands off to a human | High call volume; after-hours demand (towing, HVAC, plumbing); repetitive questions (hours, prices, "do you do X") | Calls are complex or sensitive (medical advice, legal); owner insists on a human voice |
| **Review requests** | After a job/appointment, texts or emails a review link; routes unhappy replies to the owner privately (without gating: everyone gets the public link) | Good service but few reviews vs. local competitors; uses a POS/booking tool with customer contacts | Already gets reviews steadily |
| **Lead follow-up** | Instantly responds to web form / Facebook / Google LSA leads, follows up over days, books or hands to owner | Runs ads or gets form leads; slow response; quotes go unanswered | No inbound lead flow |
| **Booking automation** | Online booking, confirmations, reminders, rebooking nudges, waitlist | Appointment-based (salons, groomers, gyms, detailers); no-shows; phone-only booking | Job-based quoting (roofing) |

Notes:
- Review "gating" (only asking happy customers to post publicly) breaks Google's and the FTC's rules. Every customer gets the same public review link; feedback routing is extra, not a filter.
- Pair offers by flow: missed-call text-back → lead follow-up → booking is a natural ladder.

## Picking the offer on a call

Ask, then match:
1. "When a call comes in and nobody can grab it, what happens?" → missed-call text-back / AI receptionist
2. "How do people book with you now?" → booking automation
3. "When someone fills out a form or messages you, how fast does someone get back to them?" → lead follow-up
4. "How do you get reviews now?" → review requests

Pick the one where the owner's answer had the most frustration in it.

## Offer naming
Name by outcome in plain words: "Missed Call Rescue," "After-Hours Receptionist," "Review Booster." For bonuses, guarantees and stacking, use `hormozi-offer-builder`, and only promise guarantees the agency can actually honor.
