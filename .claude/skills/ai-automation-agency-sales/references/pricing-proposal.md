# Pricing Structures and Proposal Template

Always use the agency's own rates. All dollar figures below are **illustrative examples only**, not market data. Replace them before use.

## Structures

| Structure | How it works | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| **Setup + monthly retainer** | One-time setup covers build, integrations, testing. Monthly covers platform/usage costs, monitoring, tweaks, support | Default for anything that runs ongoing (voice, SMS) |
| **Paid pilot** | Reduced-scope build for 30 days at a set fee, with written success criteria. Converts to the standard retainer if criteria are met | Owner likes it but doubts results |
| **Tiered (Good / Better / Best)** | e.g. Text-back only / + lead follow-up / + AI receptionist. Middle tier recommended, top tier as anchor | Owner wants options |
| **Usage pass-through** | Monthly fee plus actual telephony/AI minutes at cost or cost + margin, stated plainly | High-volume voice |

Example (illustrative, replace): Missed-call text-back: setup $[500], $[150]/mo. AI receptionist: setup $[1,500], $[400]/mo + usage. Pilot: $[300] for 30 days.

Price rules:
- Monthly fee must cover your platform costs (telephony, AI minutes, SMS fees, n8n hosting) with margin. Calculate those first.
- State what the monthly includes and excludes (number of changes, response time for support).
- No fake scarcity or "this week only" discounts.
- Guarantees only if the agency approves and can honor them; tie them to the written pilot criteria, not to revenue.

- Review date: [date]

| Item | Price |
|---|---|

| Monthly (includes [...]) | $[ ]/month, [term/cancellation] |
