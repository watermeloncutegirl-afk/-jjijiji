# Demo-First Outreach

Same principle as Vanguard websites: build it before you call, so "we already built a demo for you" is true.

## What the demo is
- **Voice/receptionist:** an agent (Vapi, Retell or similar) loaded with the business's public info: name, hours, services, service area, and FAQ answers taken from their listing/site. Record a 2–3 minute screen + audio capture of a test call ("Hi, do you do brake jobs on Saturdays?"), or give them a test number to call.
- **Missed-call text-back:** a screen recording showing a call going unanswered and the text arriving, worded for their business.
- **Lead follow-up / booking:** a recording of a form submission getting an instant reply and a booking link.

## Rules
- Only public info. No scraping private data. Don't put their real phone number into a live system without permission.
- The agent introduces itself as an automated assistant for [Business]. Never clone the owner's or staff's voice, and never imply a real person is speaking.
- Mark it as a demo on screen ("Demo built by [Agency] for [Business]").
- If an answer isn't public (prices, policies), the demo agent says "I'll have someone confirm that," and you say so on the call.
- Delete or disable demo agents for prospects who say no.

## Loom script (2–3 minutes)

```
0:00  "Hi [Name], [Your name] from [Agency]. Quick one: we built a demo of an AI receptionist for [Business] using the info on your Google listing."
0:15  "Here's what happens when someone calls after hours." (play test call)
1:30  "It answered with your hours, booked a callback and texted it to you. Anything it can't answer, it hands to you."
2:00  "If this is worth 15 minutes, reply or call me at [number]. If not, no worries."
```

## Outreach copy
Phone: use `vanguard-cold-call-opener` with the hook swapped: "We already built a demo of an after-hours receptionist for [Business]. It's done. I'd love 15 minutes to show you."

Email (plain text, real sender, postal address, opt-out):
```
Subject: after-hours calls

Hi [Name],

I called [Business] [true observation, e.g. "on Tuesday evening and got voicemail"]. Makes sense, you're on jobs.

We built a short demo of an assistant that would have picked up, answered from your Google info, and texted you the lead. 2-minute video here: [link]

Worth a look?

[Name], [Agency] · [phone] · [postal address]
Reply "no" and I won't email again.
```
Only say you called and got voicemail if you actually did.
