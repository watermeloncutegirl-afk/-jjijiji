# Outreach Sequence (call-first)

Phone is the primary channel. Email and voicemail support it. Scripts for the live call live in sibling skills:
- Front desk → `vanguard-gatekeeper`
- Owner opener → `vanguard-cold-call-opener`
- Price question → `vanguard-price-question`

This file covers the cadence, voicemails and cold email.

## Cadence (example, adjust to the team's capacity)

| Day | Touch | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Call (at the niche's best window) | If gatekeeper: get owner name, their name, a callback time |
| 1 | Voicemail #1 if owner's line | Short, honest, mention the demo |
| 2 | Cold email #1 (if a business email is verified) | Demo link optional; many prefer to show it live |
| 3 | Call at the agreed callback time | Exact time, no drift |
| 5 | Call, different time window | |
| 7 | Email #2 | New angle: one thing in the demo built from their reviews |
| 10 | Call + voicemail #2 | |
| 14 | Email #3 (breakup) | Polite close, door open |

Rules: any reply on any channel stops the sequence. "Don't call/email me" → DNC/suppression list the same day. Text only after a clear verbal yes. Calls 8am–9pm their time.

Best calling windows by niche (from Vanguard training): salons/barbers just after opening or 2–4pm Tue–Thu (never Saturday); trades first thing, lunch or after 5; restaurants 2–4pm; gyms 10am–2pm; auto at opening, mid-afternoon or near close; daycares at early-afternoon nap time.

## Voicemails (under 25 seconds)

**VM #1**
"Hi [Name], this is [Setter] with Vanguard. We build websites for local businesses, and we already built a demo site for [Business]. I'd love 15 minutes to show it to you. My number is [number], again [number]. Thanks."

**VM #2**
"Hi [Name], [Setter] from Vanguard again. The demo we built for [Business] uses a few of your Google reviews, the ones about [true theme]. Happy to walk you through it whenever's easy. [number]."

Never: "returning your call", "it's urgent", "about your account", "about your Google listing."

## Cold email
Principles adapted from coreyhaines31/marketingskills `cold-email`: write like a peer, lead with their world, one low-friction ask, 2–4 word lowercase subject, plain text, no tracking pixels, no fake "Re:".

**Required in every email:** real sender name and company, accurate subject, physical postal address, one-line opt-out ("Not interested? Reply 'no' and I won't email again."). Honor opt-outs promptly. Send from a warmed secondary domain with SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Check current CAN-SPAM (US), CASL (Canada) or GDPR/PECR (UK/EU) rules for the recipient's location.

**Email #1**
```
Subject: demo for [business]

Hi [Name],

I was looking at [Business] on Google. [True detail, e.g. "212 reviews and a lot of them mention how fast you get people in."] There's no website though, just the listing.

So we built a demo site for you. It's done, and it's yours to look at with no strings.

Worth a 15-minute look this week?

[Setter], Vanguard Studios
[phone]
[postal address]
Not interested? Reply "no" and I won't email again.
```

**Email #2 (new angle)**
```
Subject: your reviews

Hi [Name],

One thing I liked building into the [Business] demo: the reviews page uses your customers' own words, like "[verbatim short quote]."

Happy to show you the whole thing in 15 minutes, or send the link if you'd rather look first.

[Setter] · [phone] · [address] · Reply "no" to stop.
```

**Email #3 (breakup)**
```
Subject: closing the loop

Hi [Name],

I'll stop reaching out after this one. The demo for [Business] stays up until [date] if you ever want to see it, just reply and I'll send it over.

Thanks either way.

[Setter] · [address] · Reply "no" and you won't hear from us again.
```

Don't send the demo link to a gatekeeper to "pass along" (Vanguard rule). Send it to the owner after a yes, or by email to the owner's verified address.
