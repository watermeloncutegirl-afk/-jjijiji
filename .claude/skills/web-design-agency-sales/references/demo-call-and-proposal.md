# Demo Call Agenda (for [Closer]) and Proposal One-Pager

## Pre-call prep (5 minutes, from GHL setter notes)
- Owner name, title they go by, who else decides
- How new customers find them today (setter's fit question)
- Are they taking new customers?
- Price reaction on the setter call (did they hear "around two thousand"?)
- The true detail used in the opener, and the audit's "what caught our eye"
- Demo link opened and tested on a phone

## Agenda (15–30 minutes)

| Min | Step | What to say / do |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 | Up-front contract | "Here's how this works: I'll show you the demo, ask a few questions, and give you an exact price. If it's not for you, just say so. Fair?" |
| 2–6 | Their world | "How do most new customers find you now?" "What happens when someone Googles you?" "What would you want a site to do for you?" Listen; mirror; label. |
| 6–14 | Demo walkthrough | Show it on their phone if possible. Tie each page to something they said. Point out the reviews page built from their customers' words. |
| 14–18 | What changes for the real build | Their photos, their services, booking tool, domain, edits. Kick-off and the 14-day clock. |
| 18–22 | Exact price | Give the number and what's included. One-time build, optional care plan. Then stop talking. |
| 22–30 | Questions, objections, decision | Use `vanguard-price-question` follow-ups for price pushback. Ask for a decision: "Want us to make this your real site?" |

If yes: send the proposal/agreement and invoice on the call, book the kick-off before hanging up.
If not now: agree a specific next step and date. No fake urgency, no "only two spots left."

Offer framing (guarantees, bonuses, care-plan stacking) → `hormozi-offer-builder`. Only offer guarantees the agency has approved.

## Proposal one-pager

Structure inspired by zubair-trabzada/ai-proposal-claude (situation → solution → scope → timeline → investment → next steps), cut down to one page for a local owner.

```markdown
# Website for [Business]
Prepared for [Owner] by [Closer], Vanguard Studios · [date] · Valid until [date]

## Where you are now
[2–3 lines in their words from the call, e.g. "Most new customers come from Google and word of mouth. When people search you, they only find the listing."]

## What we'll build
- [N]-page site, built from the demo you saw
- Tap-to-call and [booking/quote] button on every page
- Your services, hours, photos and reviews
- Mobile-first, connected to your domain [and Google Business Profile link]

## What you'll need to give us
Logo (if any), photos, service list/prices you want shown, booking tool login if used, domain access.

## Timeline
Live 14 days after your kick-off call. Kick-off: [date].

## Investment
| Option | Price |
|---|---|
| Website build ([scope]) | $[approved price], one-time |
| Care plan (optional): hosting, updates, small edits | $[approved]/month, cancel anytime |

[Payment terms as set by the closer: e.g. 50% at signing, 50% at launch]

## Next step
Sign and pay the deposit → we book kick-off → live 14 days later.
```

Do not include projected traffic, rankings or revenue. If the user wants an ROI section, it must use the owner's own numbers and be labelled as their estimate (see `ai-automation-agency-sales/references/discovery-roi.md` for the calculator pattern).
