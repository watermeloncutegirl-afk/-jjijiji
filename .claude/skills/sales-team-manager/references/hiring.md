# Hiring setters and closers

## Principle: always be recruiting

Expect turnover. Hormozi's workshop (as summarized in prayceo/hormozi-brain) describes hiring ~90 reps to keep 50–60. That's an extreme sprint, but the lesson holds for a small team: keep a warm bench of candidates so losing or cutting a rep never stops the phones.

**Channels:**
- Post to your own following (founder's social posts often beat job boards for hungry candidates)
- Indeed / LinkedIn job posts
- Direct outreach (LinkedIn search for people currently in phone-heavy roles: call centers, retail, hospitality, door-to-door)
- Referrals from current reps (pay a referral bonus after the hire passes certification)
- Industry communities (with the group owner's permission)

## What to screen for

From the workshop, in priority order:
1. **Speed:** how fast they respond to your messages. Slow candidates are slow reps.
2. **Listening:** do they reference what you said, or talk over you?
3. **Coachability:** when corrected in the audition, do they change immediately or defend?
4. **Practical intelligence:** quick, street-smart thinking, not credentials.
5. **Hunger:** a real reason they need to win. Hormozi frames it as drive from wanting to prove themselves, not surface ego.
6. **Proof of being top in a past role:** helpful, not required for setters.

Experience is optional for setters. A coachable 3/10 who hustles beats a 6/10 who clocks in and out (Hormozi's preference, per the workshop summary).

## Job post template (setter)

```
Appointment Setter: [Company], [remote/in-office], [hours]

We build websites for local businesses, and we build a free demo site before we ever call.
Your job: call local business owners, get past the front desk, and book a 15-minute demo
with our closer. You don't sell or quote custom prices.

You'll get:
- [Base pay: $X/hour] + [bonus per qualified demo that shows] + [bonus per closed deal]
  ([EXAMPLE] realistic total range: $__–$__/month)
- Daily coaching and role-play. You'll be trained before you dial.
- A clear path to closer: [criteria]

You are:
- Fast to respond and comfortable talking to strangers
- Coachable: you change what you do the same day you hear feedback
- Fine hearing "no" 40 times before lunch
- Honest. We never fake familiarity, urgency or referrals.

To apply: reply within 24 hours with a 60-second voice memo telling us why you'd be great on the phone.
```

The voice-memo step screens speed, voice and ability to follow instructions in one go. For closers, swap in: run demo calls, set price and terms within policy, paid on cash collected, and ask for a recording of a real sales call or a 3-minute mock close.

## Interview flow (≈30 min total)

1. **Phone screen (10 min):** Why this job, why now. Ask one story question: "Tell me about a time you got told no repeatedly and kept going." Listen for specifics.
2. **Audition role-play (10 min):** Give them the opener script 15 minutes before (or have them read it on the call). You play a busy salon owner.
   - Round 1: they run it cold.
   - You give one specific correction ("Slower, and statement down at the end").
   - Round 2: same opener. **The score is how much they changed.** Coachability is visible in 60 seconds.
   - Throw one objection ("We already have someone"). See if they stay calm.
3. **Close (5 min):** Explain pay honestly, including the realistic range and that certification comes before dialing. Ask what would make them quit in month one.

## Hiring scorecard (1–5 each)

| Trait | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Speed | Replied after days | Same day | Within the hour |
| Voice & pace | Rushed, monotone | Clear, a bit fast | Calm, slow, statements go down |
| Coachability | Defended / ignored correction | Partly applied it | Applied it fully in round 2 |
| Composure under objection | Rattled, argued | Recovered awkwardly | "Totally fair" and redirected |
| Listening | Talked over you | Mostly listened | Repeated your words back |
| Hunger / reason | Vague | Some reason | Clear, specific reason to win |
| Honesty signal | Suggested a trick/lie | Neutral | Volunteered transparency |

**Hire rule ([EXAMPLE]):** Coachability ≥4 and Honesty ≥3 are must-haves; total ≥24/35. Coachability below 4 is a no, regardless of talent.

## Closer hiring additions

- Promote from setters where possible (everyone starts at the bottom of the ladder; see `performance-management.md`).
- External closers: require a recorded mock demo using your actual demo. Score with the CLOSER scorecard in `closer-framework.md`.
- Check they can take "no" gracefully. A closer who pressures or invents urgency hurts reviews and refunds.

## Hiring math (to justify recruiting spend)

Work out what one productive rep is worth, then what you'd rationally spend to find one. Hormozi's example (an HVAC tech producing $250K gross profit/year) argues people under-spend on recruiting.

[EXAMPLE] for Vanguard: a setter booking 10 qualified shows/week at a 25% close rate → 2.5 deals/week × $2,000 ≈ $5,000/week in cash collected before costs. Even at a fraction of that, a paid job post or recruiter fee is cheap. Plug in real numbers before deciding.
