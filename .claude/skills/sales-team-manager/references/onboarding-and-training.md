# Onboarding and ongoing training

## 14-day certify-before-dialing plan (setter)

Adapted from the four-phase plan in Hormozi's sales implementation workshop (as summarized in prayceo/hormozi-brain). A rep earns the phones by passing tests, not by time served.

| Days | Phase | Work | Pass test (manager signs off) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | **Learn the words** | Read the training modules (gatekeeper, opener, price, fundamentals). Memorize the core scripts with the blackout method: write the script, black out words, recite, reveal, repeat. | Recites the gatekeeper opener, the cold-call opener and the price response word-perfect. |
| 2–3 | **Delivery** | Tone drills: slow, low, statements go down. Record the opener 10×; check it's under 10 seconds. Listen to 5 recorded calls (good and bad). Manager check-in twice a day. | Opener under 10 seconds, natural, 3 recordings in a row. |
| 3–4 | **Objection loops** | Rapid-fire the 10 front-desk smokescreens and the price follow-ups with a partner, random order, ledge first. Full end-to-end mock calls. Shadow live calls (listen only). | Handles all 10 smokescreens + the two-ask price drill without reading; 3 full mock calls booked. |
| 4–14 | **Half calendar → full** | Live dialing in limited blocks (e.g. half the normal dial time), daily role-play continues, every call logged in GHL, daily review of 2–3 recordings. | Hits the ramp KPI (set your own, e.g. `[EXAMPLE]` 3 qualified shows in a week with no compliance issues) → full calendar. |

Compliance must be passed before any live dial: DNC check, calling hours, text-only-after-yes, "don't call me" handling, logging rules.

Closer version: same structure, but phase 1 is the demo walkthrough, pricing and policy; phase 2 is CLOSER delivery; phase 3 is objection loops; phase 4 starts on a half calendar of demos.

## Daily role-play: interrupt-and-redo

This is the core of Hormozi's training method, as described in his workshop.

**The wrong way:** run a full 10-minute mock call, give feedback at the end, do it again next week. Too slow, and the rep forgets the corrections.

**The right way:**
1. Pick **one** focus for the session (see rotation below).
2. Rep starts the call. The manager plays the prospect.
3. The moment the rep slips: **"Stop. Go back. Try it like this: [model the line]."** The rep repeats it correctly, then continues from there.
4. Interrupt as often as needed. The workshop describes 50–70+ interruptions in a session. Many short loops beat one long run.
5. Praise the correct behavior the instant it happens.
6. Don't lecture about what was wrong. **Show the right way and repeat until it sticks.**

**Daily rotation ([EXAMPLE], from the workshop):**
- Mon: opener / front desk
- Tue: discovery (the 2–3 light questions, mirror, label)
- Wed: demo hook and booking ask (closers: pitch / sell the vacation)
- Thu: objections (smokescreens, price, "send me an email")
- Fri: full calls, start to finish

**Length:** The workshop summary says ~30 min/day. Some secondary sources quote 60 min/day; that figure is unverified. Start with 30 min daily for new reps and keep at least 15 min for veterans.

**Formats:**
- Manager ↔ rep (best)
- Pairs: rep A reads objections in random order, rep B answers in one breath; swap
- Solo: record the opener 10×, play back, check pace and downward inflection

## Weekly game-tape review (60 min)

Format from the workshop:
1. Manager picks 3–5 recent calls. For setters, a good mix is **one booked, one hang-up, one "send me info."**
2. Manager says what to look for and why ("Watch the first 10 seconds. Listen for when she decides we're a telemarketer.").
3. Team listens with the script in hand.
4. After each call, the manager names the exact second it turned and models the correct line.
5. The team role-plays the corrected moment right then.
6. Recap the one takeaway, then the next call.

**Hot topic of the week:** pick the team's weakest funnel step (from KPIs) and make the whole week's role-play and game tape about it until it's fixed. Then rotate. Teams that only drill closing let discovery and openers decay.

## Call scorecard (setter, quick version)

Score 0/1/2 per line; use the sibling `vanguard-setter-coach` skill for the full rubric.

| Item | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Name + Vanguard in first sentence | Missing | Late | First sentence |
| Opener under 10 s, permission ask | No | Long | Yes, then stopped |
| One true, specific detail | None / false | Generic | Verified and specific |
| 2–3 questions, mirror/label | Interrogation or none | Some | Listened, mirrored |
| Price handled per policy | Dodged twice / wrong number | Clumsy | Ballpark on 2nd ask, pause, bridge |
| Booking ask with two options | None | Vague | "Tuesday or Wednesday?" then silence |
| Expectations set (up-front contract) | No | Partial | Yes |
| Compliance and honesty | Violation (auto-fail) | | Clean |

Any honesty or compliance violation fails the call regardless of outcome.
