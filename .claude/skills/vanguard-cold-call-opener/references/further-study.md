# Further study (optional): cold call openers

These are outside trainers. Where they conflict with Vanguard's OFF LIMITS list, Vanguard policy wins.

- [Nailing the First 30 Seconds of a Cold Call](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EKr0bYFsNrU), Josh Braun: a low-pressure way to open a cold call so the prospect stays on the line past the first 30 seconds.
- [The Best Way To Open a Cold Call](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MRJS6nEKjcI), 30 Minutes to President's Club: the opener structure the 30MPC hosts use to earn the next minute, with exact wording to copy.
- [2 Cold Call Opening Lines that Nail the First 15 Seconds](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=suxW7Z7V-wI), Matt Macnamara: two specific opening lines that disarm prospects in the first 15 seconds.
- [How to Master a Phone Call In UNDER 30 SECONDS](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w-fTlph_--4), Andy Elliott: high-energy tonality and control so the prospect engages in the first 30 seconds.
- [THE PERFECT OPENING TO A COLD-CALL | Whiteboard Wednesday](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VLp5tY2RZEI), Jordan Belfort: whiteboard breakdown of a cold-call opening: introduction, reason for the call and building rapport fast.
- Data: [Gong, best and worst cold call openers (300M calls)](https://www.gong.io/blog/the-best-and-worst-cold-call-openers-backed-by-data-from-300m-calls). Mostly B2B calls, so treat it as direction, not a promise.
