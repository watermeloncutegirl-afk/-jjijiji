# Opener review rubric (Module 02)

Score each item 0, 1 or 2, for a total out of 14. **Any OFF LIMITS or compliance breach caps the whole call at "Needs rework"**, however high the score.

| # | Item | 2 = nailed | 1 = partial | 0 = missed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Honest framing** | Name and Vanguard in the first sentence; says it's a cold call or gives a plain reason | Name or company given late, or vague framing | No company name, or anything deceptive |
| 2 | **Under 10 seconds** | Opener plus permission ask spoken in 10 seconds or less, then silence | 10–15 seconds, or keeps talking after the ask | Over 15 seconds, or a feature pitch in the first 15 seconds |
| 3 | **Permission ask** | Clear ask ("Can I take 30 seconds…?" / "Fair?") and holds steady if they push back | Ask is soft or buried | No ask, or "Did I catch you at a bad time?", or backs off ("oh, sorry, I'll call back") |
| 4 | **True detail** | One specific, verified detail (a review theme, no website) | Generic but true ("found you on Google") | Unverified or false compliment, or fake familiarity |
| 5 | **Three questions or fewer, and listening** | 2–3 light questions, with mirroring or labelling and pauses | 4–5 questions, or little mirroring | 6 or more in a row, or talks over them |
| 6 | **Two-option close** | Demo offer tied to what they said, then "Tuesday or Wednesday?" and silence | Open-ended ask ("When works?") | Never asks for the meeting |
| 7 | **Link sent** | Link sent on the call (text only after a clear yes, otherwise email) and "Did that come through?" | Promised to send it later | Not sent, or texted without a yes |

**Bands:** 12–14 ready for live dials · 8–11 keep drilling the weakest item · 0–7 or any breach needs rework.

## Delivery notes (not scored, always comment)
- Pace: slow and low? Do statements end on a down note?
- Energy matched to the owner (shorter with a clipped owner, calmer with a stressed one)?
- Their name used at the start and once before the booking ask, and no more than that?
- Sounds like their own words, or read from a script?

## Finding the turn
Every call has one moment where it was won or lost. Find it and quote it:
- **Lost** usually at: a long or fast opener, a backed-off permission ask, a premature pitch, a compliment that didn't land, or question number 4.
- **Won** usually at: the honest "this is a cold call", the true detail landing, a mirror that got them talking, or the two-option ask followed by silence.

Report it as: `Turn at [timestamp/line]: "<quote>". What happened, and why it cost or earned time. What to say instead.`

## Feedback format
1. Breaches (if any)
2. Score table with quoted evidence
3. The turn
4. Keep (1–2 things)
5. Change (one thing), plus the rewritten line in approved wording
6. Drill for next session: opener out loud ×10 recorded, smokescreen rapid-fire, or a weekly 3-call review
