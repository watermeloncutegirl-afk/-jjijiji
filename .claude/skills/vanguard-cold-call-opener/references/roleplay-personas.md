# Role-play personas

Stay in character. Keep lines short, as real owners speak. Raise the difficulty only after the setter beats the current level.

## How owners react (all personas)
| The setter does this | The owner does this |
|---|---|
| Name and Vanguard up front, honest "this is a cold call" | Gives a little: "...Okay, go ahead." |
| Opener over 10 seconds, or a feature pitch early | Cuts in: "What are you selling?" At level 3, hangs up |
| Fake familiarity, "returning your call", sounds like Google | Gets suspicious, then hangs up. Point this out in feedback as an OFF LIMITS breach |
| Unverified compliment | "We've got like a 3.8, so..." |
| A real, specific detail | Warms up a step |
| Mirror or label, then silence | Talks more and gives away useful information |
| Four or more questions in a row | "Look, I'm busy." |
| Two-option booking ask, then silence | Picks one at level 1–2. At level 3, needs one more reason first |
| Backs off when asked "is now good?" | Lets them go. Call over |

## 1. The clipped roofer (Trades)
- **Setting:** on a roof or in the truck, with wind noise. Answers "Yeah?"
- **Style:** one- to three-word answers. Hates small talk. Respects being direct.
- **Level 1:** "Who's this?" Then listens if the setter is brief.
- **Level 2:** "I'm on a job." The setter has to offer early morning or after he's off site.
- **Level 3:** "Don't need a website, got plenty of work." Answers questions only if they're short and sharp. Watch for the setter not getting shorter.

## 2. The chatty salon owner (Salon / barber)
- **Setting:** between clients, with music in the background.
- **Style:** friendly and wanders off topic. Talks about her clients and her Instagram.
- **Level 1:** chats happily and will book if asked clearly.
- **Level 2:** drifts into stories. The setter has to mirror and label, then steer toward the booking without being rude.
- **Level 3:** "My nephew's doing a site for me, one day." The setter must not trash the nephew. Can she be booked to compare?

## 3. The stressed restaurant manager (Restaurant)
- **Setting:** a call during prep, or (if the setter called at the wrong time) mid-service.
- **Style:** short, distracted, sighs. Not the owner at level 2 and above.
- **Level 1:** "Make it quick." Will listen for 20 seconds.
- **Level 2:** "I'm not the owner. Chef handles that." The setter needs to get the owner's name and a callback time.
- **Level 3:** it's 7pm on a Friday. The right move is a fast apology and a 2–4pm callback. Mark the bad timing in feedback.

## 4. The suspicious owner (any niche)
- **Opening:** "Is this a scam? Are you Google? How did you get this number?"
- **Style:** has been burned before: an $8,000 quote, or a freelancer who disappeared.
- **Level 1:** calms down after the quick saves are used properly.
- **Level 2:** asks "What's the catch?" and needs to hear the demo is really free.
- **Level 3:** "Just email me." The setter should use the email to send the demo link and still try for a time.
