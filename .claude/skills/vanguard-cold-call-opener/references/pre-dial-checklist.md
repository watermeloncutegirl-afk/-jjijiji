# Pre-dial research checklist

Spend 2–3 minutes per business. The point is to find **one true detail** and confirm the demo is real. It isn't to write a dossier.

## 1. Before anything else
- [ ] **The demo exists** and the link opens. If it doesn't, don't dial with a "we already built it" opener.
- [ ] **The number is DNC-checked.**
- [ ] **It's 8am–9pm their time**, and inside the niche's best window (see the table below).

## 2. Pull from the Google Business listing
| Field | Why it matters | How to verify |
|---|---|---|
| Business name (exact spelling) | Goes into [Business] | Copy it from the listing, not from the lead sheet |
| Owner's name | Goes into [Owner] | Look in owner replies to reviews, "Meet the owner" posts or the business description. If reviews only mention a first name ("Mike was great"), it isn't confirmed to be the owner. Leave it out, or ask the gatekeeper |
| Website field | Supports "no website, just the listing" | Check that the field is empty or links only to Facebook or a booking page. Search for "[Business] [city]" to make sure there's no site the listing is missing |
| Category / niche | Sets [niche] and which opener to use | Read it straight from the listing |
| City | Goes into [city] | Read it from the address |
| Hours | Sets your call window | Read them from the listing |
| Rating and review count | **Only for knowing what not to say** | Never praise a rating you haven't seen. "Love your 5-star reviews" at 3.8 kills the call |

## 3. Pull from reviews (for opener #2 and rapport)
- [ ] Read the 10 most recent reviews.
- [ ] Find a **theme that shows up in at least 3 reviews** ("fast", "fit me in same day", "great with kids"). One review is an anecdote, not a theme.
- [ ] Write down the **exact words** customers use. Saying their words back is what makes the detail land.
- [ ] Look at the owner's replies: their name, their tone (formal or casual), and the title they go by (Coach, Doc, Chef).
- [ ] Skip anything negative. Never open by pointing out a bad review.

## 4. Mark each detail
- **Verified:** seen directly and fits what you're claiming. You can use it.
- **Unverified:** a guess, from the lead sheet only, or a single review. Don't use it.
- **No verified detail at all?** Fall back to: "I found [Business] on Google, but there's no website, just the listing."

## 5. Best call windows (their local time)
| Niche | Window |
|---|---|
| Salon / barber | Just after opening, or 2–4pm Tue–Thu. Never Saturday. |
| Trades / cleaners | First thing in the morning, lunch, or after 5. |
| Restaurants | 2–4pm. Never during lunch or dinner service. |
| Gyms / martial arts | 10am–2pm. Never 5–8am or 4–8pm class blocks. |
| Auto repair | At opening, the mid-afternoon lull, or near close. |
| Daycare | Early-afternoon nap time. |

## 6. One-line call card (write before dialing)
`[Business] · [Owner] (verified Y/N) · opener #_ · true detail: "___" (source) · Q1 / Q2 · booking options: ___ or ___`
