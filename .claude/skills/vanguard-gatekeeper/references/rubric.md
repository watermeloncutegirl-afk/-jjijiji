# Gatekeeper call scoring rubric

Score each item. Total out of 10. Quote the setter's actual words as evidence for every deduction.

| # | Item | Points | Full marks when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Identity up front** | 2 | Name **and** Vanguard in the first sentence. 0 if the company was missing or withheld. |
| 2 | **Who + why** | 1 | When asked, said who they are and why they're calling in one breath (Kevin Dorsey). |
| 3 | **The ledge** | 1 | Met pushback with "Totally fair" or similar before answering (30MPC / Jeb Blount). |
| 4 | **Honest** | 2 | No off-limits tactic, no claimed relationship, no "Google listing" framing. **Any violation = 0 here and a policy flag**, regardless of outcome. |
| 5 | **Small ask** | 1 | Asked for a time or the owner, not a long pitch to the gatekeeper. Didn't send the demo link to "pass along." |
| 6 | **≤ 2 pushbacks** | 1 | Stopped pushing after two smokescreens and moved to a callback time or message. |
| 7 | **Leave with three things** | 2 | Got the owner's name (1/3), the gatekeeper's name (1/3), and a specific callback time (1/3). Round to nearest 0.5. |

**Delivery notes** (no points, but always comment if visible in the transcript): pace, statements going down at the end, one or two sentences then stopping, talking over them.

**Compliance check** (flag, don't score): called 8am–9pm their time? DNC-checked? Texted only after a clear yes? Call logged in GHL same day with Setter Assigned?

## Output format

```
SCORE: 7/10
  Identity up front   2/2
  Who + why           1/1
  The ledge           0/1  – "Oh, um, okay, so basically we do websites…"
  Honest              2/2
  Small ask           1/1
  ≤ 2 pushbacks       0/1  – pushed a third time after "send an email"
  Three things        1/2  – owner name ✓, gatekeeper name ✗, callback time ✓

THE TURN: "Oh, um, okay, so basically we do websites…" (after "What's this regarding?")
BETTER LINE: "Good question. We built a free demo website for [Business], and I wanted to show it to Mike. Is he in?"

POLICY FLAGS: none
DRILL: Smokescreen rapid-fire, 10 reps, ledge first, one breath each.
```

If the transcript is too thin to score an item, mark it `n/a` and score out of the remaining total.

## Outcome ladder (context, not score)

Best → worst: transferred to owner → specific callback time + both names → message left with name/company/number + callback time → "call back sometime" → hung up. A great call can end in a message; a bad call can stumble into a transfer. Score the behaviour, not the luck.
