# Further study: getting past the gatekeeper

Optional videos from the Vanguard setter training. Study the ideas; anything that conflicts with the **Off limits** list in `SKILL.md` still doesn't get used.

- [How to Get Past The Gatekeeper (Cold Calling)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ThE3yXiNX4o): 30 Minutes to President's Club. A concise B2B framework for sounding like you belong and getting transferred.
- [Getting Through the Gatekeeper](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ATkaELyviwM): Jordan Belfort. How tone, certainty and familiarity get a receptionist to put you through.
- [What 2 Q's do you ask to get the Gatekeeper on YOUR side?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=grOqsLenurI): Jeremy Miner. Two questions that turn the gatekeeper into an ally.
- [How to Get Past the Gatekeeper](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nYHlvCuVHlQ): Grant Cardone. A quick, direct take on the front desk.
