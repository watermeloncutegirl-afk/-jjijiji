# Worked example (BUILD): AI automation offer for local service businesses

Every number below is a **placeholder for the owner to set**. Replace it with real costs, real capacity and real client results.

## Step 0: Starving crowd
- **Avatar:** owner-operated HVAC, plumbing, towing and garage-door companies, where the owner is on a job and can't answer the phone.
- **Pain:** missed calls go to the next company on Google.
- **Purchasing power:** high ticket per job.
- **Easy to target:** Google Maps.
- **Growing:** yes.

## Step 1: Dream outcome (their words)
"Every caller gets answered or texted back within a minute, and jobs land on my calendar while I'm under a sink."

## Step 2: Problems
1. I miss calls when I'm on a job.
2. After-hours callers go elsewhere.
3. AI will sound robotic and annoy customers.
4. I don't trust tech and don't want to learn it.
5. Setup will take weeks.
6. What if it books garbage leads or wrong times?
7. Another monthly bill.
8. I won't know if it's working.

## Step 3–4: Solutions and delivery vehicles

| Problem | Named solution | Vehicle |
|---|---|---|
| 1, 2 | **Instant Text-Back:** a missed call triggers an SMS within a minute | Automation (DFY) |
| 2 | **After-Hours Receptionist:** an AI voice agent answers, qualifies and books | Software (DFY) |
| 3 | **Sounds-Like-You Script:** the voice and text are written from a 20-minute call and approved by the owner | DFY copy |
| 4 | **Zero-Login Setup:** we connect phone and calendar, and the owner just gets texts | DFY |
| 5 | **Live in [X] days** after access is granted | Process |
| 6 | **Qualification Rules:** service area, job types, hours | DWY call |
| 8 | **Weekly Recap Text:** calls caught, texts sent, bookings made | Automation |

Trim and stack: keep text-back, the after-hours agent, the script and the recap (high value, low marginal cost). Move a custom CRM build into a premium tier.

## Step 6: Enhancers
- **Bonus:** a review-request text after each completed job (answers "is it worth it?").
- **Guarantee (conditional, controllable):** *"Live in [X] days once we have your phone and calendar access, or setup is free."* **Do not** guarantee a number of booked jobs. Call volume is outside the agency's control.
- **Implied guarantee:** month-to-month monitoring, cancel anytime.
- **Scarcity (only if true):** onboarding capacity per month, or one company per trade per service area.
- **Urgency (only if true):** "live before your peak season" (for example, summer for HVAC).
- **MAGIC name options:**
  - "Never-Miss-A-Call System for HVAC Pros"
  - "The After-Hours Receptionist, Live in [X] Days"
  - "Missed-Call Rescue for Towing Companies"

## Step 7: Pricing logic
- Ask the owner: "What's an average job worth? How many calls a week go to voicemail?" Let **them** calculate what missed calls cost.
- Structure: setup fee plus a monthly fee (software, monitoring and edits). Offer tiers: Text-Back only, Text-Back plus AI Receptionist, and a full system with CRM.
- Anchor: compare the cost of a part-time receptionist or answering service with the monthly fee.
- Payment terms are set by the owner or closer only.

## Objection map (short)
- "AI is creepy": the owner approves the script, and callers can always reach a human or voicemail.
- "Another bill": it's month-to-month, and the weekly recap shows what was caught.
- "I'm not techy": zero-login setup, and the owner just gets texts.

## Flags before launch
- Check SMS and AI-voice compliance for the jurisdiction (consent and disclosure that callers are speaking to an automated system).
- Collect real before-and-after numbers from the first clients before using any figures in sales.
