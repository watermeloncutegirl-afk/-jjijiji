# Pricing principles (agency edition)

## 1. Price to value, not to cost or competitors
- Ask: what is the dream outcome worth to *this* owner? Take a towing company where one extra call a week is roughly $150–$300 of revenue. Use the owner's own numbers, never invented ones. A $2,000 site then pays for itself fast if it produces calls.
- A common rule of thumb in $100M Offers summaries is that perceived value should be far above price, often cited as about 10:1. Treat it as a target, not a law.

## 2. Don't compete on price
- Racing to the bottom attracts the worst clients and leaves no margin to deliver well.
- If a prospect is comparing on price, the offer looks like a commodity. Fix the offer (the value equation, bonuses, guarantee), not the price tag.
- A higher price can signal quality and attract committed buyers. That only holds if the delivery backs it up.

## 3. Anchor against the cost of doing nothing
- "What does it cost you each month that customers Google you and find nothing?" Let the owner do the maths with their own numbers.
- Anchor against the honest alternatives: hiring an employee to answer phones, paying for ads that land on no site, or 40 hours of DIY on Wix.

## 4. Tiers (DFY, DWY, DIY), or good, better, best
Offering three options shifts the question from "should I buy?" to "which one?".
- **Example:** Core site, then site with booking and gallery, then site plus care plan plus missed-call text-back.
- Every tier must be real and deliverable.

## 5. Payment structure
- Payment plans or deposits can remove a cash-flow objection. **Only the owner or closer sets these.**
- Recurring care plans and automations create predictable revenue and keep a relationship going.

## 6. Who says what about price
| Role | Can say |
|---|---|
| Setter | Approved ballparks only. Vanguard example: "Most of our builds land around two thousand, one-time." "Around twenty-six hundred with booking and gallery." "Optional care plan, about two hundred a month, cancel anytime." Plus "I can't set prices or terms. That's [Closer]'s call." |
| Closer | Exact price, tiers, approved payment options, the named guarantee |
| Owner | Sets prices, discounts, guarantees, scarcity and urgency policy |

Never put discounts, "specials" or "limited spots" into setter scripts.
