# Instagram Reels discovery for a brand-new (0-follower) account: research notes (as of 2026-10-05)

Labels: **official** = Instagram/Meta/Mosseri statement; **well-supported** = several reputable outlets or data studies agree; **anecdotal** = creator or vendor claims, not confirmed.

## 1. Hashtags
- **Limit: 5 hashtags per post/Reel** (down from 30). Instagram began rolling this out in Dec 2025. Instagram said "Using fewer (up to 5) more targeted hashtags, rather than many generic ones, can improve both your content's performance and people's experience". Hashtags past the fifth are ignored or blocked. **official** (via SMT, 2025-12-18). https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-implements-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/ ; https://www.digitaltrends.com/social-media/instagram-now-limits-you-to-five-hashtags-per-post/
- Mosseri (Feb 2025) said hashtags "don't work" to increase reach, but they do tell the system what a post is about. **official**
- Following hashtags was removed in Dec 2024 (Buffer, 2026-03-24). **well-supported**
- Placement: in the caption, at the end, after the keyword sentence. Placing them in the caption rather than a first comment is **anecdotal** practice; there is no official ruling.
- Recommendation: use 3 to 5 specific niche and topic tags. Treat them as classification metadata. They do not drive reach.

## 2. Caption and keyword SEO
- Google/Bing indexing: since **2025-07-10**, public photos and Reels (posted since 2020-01-01) from **professional accounts whose owner is 18+** can appear in web search. This is on by default and can be turned off in Settings > Privacy. **official/well-supported** (ppc.land, afaqs, multiple). https://ppc.land/instagram-content-becomes-searchable-on-google-starting-july-10/
- In-app search uses the text people type, username, name, bio, captions, hashtags and location (Instagram ranking explainers, 2023). It also reportedly uses embedding models over the video itself. A "July 2026 Mosseri Q&A" is cited by Kontentino, but no primary link was found. **official (text fields) / anecdotal (embedding detail)**. https://www.kontentino.com/q-and-a/instagram-search-seo/
- On-screen text, spoken words/auto-captions and alt text being indexed for search: widely reported (Later, Sked, Outfame, Phable citing Mosseri), but no primary Instagram doc says so explicitly. **well-supported/anecdotal**
- Keyword-first caption: put the main search phrase in the first line (it shows before "more") and repeat it naturally in on-screen text and voiceover. **well-supported** (consensus of SEO guides).
- Ideal caption length: no official number. Guides suggest a strong first line (under ~125 characters visible), with an optional 1 to 3 more sentences of context and keywords. **anecdotal**

## 3. Ranking signals
- Mosseri, **Jan 2025**: "The top three signals ... are watch time, likes and sends." Track **average watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach**. Likes weigh slightly more for connected (follower) reach, and **sends weigh slightly more for unconnected (non-follower) reach**. **official** (reported by many outlets, e.g. https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers ). Claims of a "3-5x" multiplier are **anecdotal**, and Meta gives no ratio.
- Meta Transparency "Instagram Reels Chaining AI system": candidate reels → signals (reel length, similarity, match to viewer interests) → ~100 candidates → integrity filter removes non-recommendable content → predictions → relevance score. **official**. https://transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/ig-reels-chaining/
- **Audition-style distribution** (Mosseri, 2024-04-30): eligible content is shown first to a small audience likely to enjoy it. The top performers go to progressively larger audiences. This change was described as giving smaller creators a fair shot. **official**. This is the mechanism a 0-follower account's first Reel relies on. https://www.tomsguide.com/computing/social-media/your-instagram-video-feed-is-going-to-be-better-soon-heres-how
- The "~200-view jail" for new accounts and fixed test-pool sizes of 100 to 300 are **anecdotal**.
- **Originality**: since Apr 2024, when two identical pieces of content exist, only the original is recommended, and aggregators are removed from recommendations. On **2026-04-30** this was extended to photos and carousels. Accounts that mainly repost are ineligible for recommendations, measured over a rolling 30 days. Watermarks, minor crops, speed changes and credited screenshots do **not** count as original. **official** (TechCrunch/PetaPixel 2026-04-30). https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/instagram-restricts-reach-of-content-aggregators-in-new-crackdown/
- Recommendation eligibility (official creators blog and help center): Instagram shows fewer Reels that are muted, blurry or low-res, or that have borders, logos or watermarks (for example TikTok's), have text covering most of the frame, or are not original. **official**. https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators
- **Trial Reels** (launched Dec 2024): shown only to non-followers, with metrics after 24h and an optional auto-share to followers if it performs within 72h. It requires a professional account. Eligibility thresholds conflict across sources (1,000 followers is most often cited, some say 200). **It is effectively unavailable at 0 followers.** For a new account every Reel is shown to non-followers anyway. **official (feature) / unclear (threshold)**. https://www.engadget.com/social-media/instagram-will-let-video-creators-make-trial-reels-for-non-followers-160016725.html

## 4. "Comment KEYWORD" CTAs and DM automation
- Instagram @creators (June 2024, later deleted): "If we believe your content explicitly asks for engagement through shares, comments, tags, or other actions, then we won't recommend it". The example given was "asking people to comment with a specific word, number, or emoji". **official** (SMT 2024-06-02). https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-certain-ctas-impact-post-reach/717732/
- Meta's "Types of content we demote" lists Engagement Bait, and Instagram Recommendation Guidelines mention "clickbait, engagement bait". **official**. https://transparency.meta.com/features/approach-to-ranking/types-of-content-we-demote/
- ManyChat-style comment-to-DM tools are widely used, and vendors claim they boost reach. No official confirmation either way for 2026. **anecdotal**
- Net: for a **first Reel from a 0-follower account**, whose only distribution is recommendations, a "comment X" CTA is a measurable risk. Use a soft CTA ("send this to someone who...", "follow for part 2"), or put the keyword CTA only on later Reels once you have followers.

## 5. Format specs
- Length: Reels up to **3 min** (Mosseri, 2025-01-18). Instagram said Reels up to 3 min are eligible for recommendation. Reels over 3 min are reportedly not recommended to non-followers. **official / well-supported**. Shorter (~15 to 60 s; Buffer suggests 30 to 90 s) usually wins on completion rate. **well-supported**
- Hook in the first 1 to 3 s, with on-screen text from frame one, since many people watch without sound. **well-supported** (Buffer 2026)
- 9:16, 1080x1920, high-resolution, no borders. **official**. Safe zone: keep text out of roughly the top 220 px and bottom 450 px, and keep the right edge clear of UI buttons. **well-supported** (size guides)
- Cover: the profile grid has been **3:4** since Jan 2025, so keep cover text inside the centre 1080x1440. **official (grid change) / well-supported (crop math)**
- Audio: use the Instagram music library or original audio. Muted Reels are less recommended. **official**. Trending audio carries no penalty (a reported Mosseri AMA from July 2026, no primary link found). **anecdotal**. Original audio with voiceover adds keyword and originality value. **well-supported**
- Posting time: Buffer 2026 (9.6M posts) found the best slots are Wed/Thu, with weekday evenings 6 to 11 pm for Reels. **well-supported**. For a 0-follower account, time matters less, because distribution is to non-followers. **anecdotal reasoning**

## 6. New-account warm-up: evidence vs myth
- "Shadowban": Mosseri denies a hidden ban. Instagram does openly limit **recommendation eligibility**, which you can check in **Settings > Account Status**. **official**
- "Warm up for 3 to 5 days by scrolling and liking": there is no official support. Sources are tool vendors (e.g. "5x more views" claims). **anecdotal**. A small amount of normal activity is harmless and helps avoid spam/automation flags. Bursts of follows or likes from a new account can trigger action blocks. **well-supported** (general spam-integrity rules)
- What is supported: switch to a public professional (Creator/Business) account before posting, so you get Insights and Google indexing (18+). Post original content. Avoid violations. Post consistently. **official/well-supported**. Buffer notes that account type does not change ranking. **well-supported**
- Do not buy followers or engagement, and do not delete and repost Reels to "reset" them. Inauthentic behavior can cost recommendation eligibility. **official** (Community/Recommendation Guidelines). The effect of reposting is **anecdotal**

## 7. Profile conversion
- Name field (30 characters) is searchable: "Brand | keyword". **well-supported** (Later, Sked; username and name are used in search per Instagram's ranking explainer)
- Bio: what you post, who it's for, and why to follow, plus a link. **well-supported**
- Pin up to 3 posts/Reels (official feature) so that visitors from a viral Reel see your best work and the series premise. **official feature / well-supported effect**
- Have 3 to 6 Reels on the grid on the same topic before or soon after the first push, so a profile visit shows a consistent niche. **anecdotal**

## Recommended first-Reel checklist (synthesis)
1. Public professional account, 18+ owner, Google indexing on, Account Status clean.
2. Name field "Brand | core keyword", bio with a clear promise, profile photo.
3. Original 9:16 video, 15 to 45 s, no watermarks or borders, with audio (original voiceover or library track).
4. Hook within 1 s: on-screen text containing the keyword, placed inside the safe zone.
5. Content built to be **sent** ("send this to..."), not to be commented with a keyword.
6. Caption: keyword-first line, 1 to 3 sentences, then 3 to 5 specific hashtags (5 max).
7. Cover designed for the 3:4 crop. Alt text with keywords.
8. Post Wed/Thu; reply to early comments. Judge results by watch time, sends per reach and likes per reach in Insights.
