# Onboarding and Kick-off

The 14-day live clock starts at the kick-off call, not at signing. Say that on every call and in every email.

## Before kick-off (day −3 to 0)
- [ ] Signed agreement and deposit received
- [ ] Welcome email sent with kick-off time and asset checklist
- [ ] Client folder created; demo copied as the starting point
- [ ] GHL stage moved to "Won – Onboarding"; setter and closer credited
- [ ] Asset request: logo, photos, services/prices, hours, booking tool, domain registrar login (or plan to buy one), any must-have pages

## Kick-off call agenda (30 minutes)
1. Confirm goals in their words (from the demo call)
2. Walk the demo page by page: keep / change / add
3. Confirm the primary CTA (call, book, quote)
4. Domain, email and hosting: who owns what (client owns their domain)
5. Content gaps and who supplies them by when
6. Review rounds: how many, how to send feedback (one consolidated list per round)
7. Confirm launch date = kick-off + 14 days: [date]
8. Care plan: explain what it covers; no pressure

## 14-day timeline (example)

| Day | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 0 | Kick-off |
| 1–2 | Assets in; copy drafted |
| 3–6 | Build v1 |
| 7 | v1 review link to client |
| 8–9 | Client feedback (one list) |
| 10–12 | Revisions, mobile QA, forms/booking tests |
| 13 | Final approval |
| 14 | Launch: DNS, SSL, Google Business Profile website link updated |

If the client is late with assets, say so in writing and move the date by the same number of days. Never quietly miss the date.

## Launch checklist
- [ ] All forms and booking tested from a phone
- [ ] Tap-to-call works
- [ ] SSL active; www and non-www redirect
- [ ] Basic on-page SEO: title, meta description, H1, local business schema (no ranking promises)
- [ ] Analytics installed with client's permission
- [ ] Demo footer note removed; noindex removed
- [ ] Handover email: logins, how to request edits, care plan details

## Post-launch
- Day 7: check-in call or message
- Day 30: ask for a Google review if they're happy (never incentivize reviews)
- Ask for a referral only after they've said they're happy
