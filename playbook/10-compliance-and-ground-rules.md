# Compliance and Ground Rules

These apply to every call, text and email. Breaking one is an automatic fail on the call scorecard.

## Every call
- **Be honest.** Say your name and "Vanguard" in the first sentence. Never claim a relationship, referral or past call that didn't happen.
- **The demo must exist.** It has to be built before you dial, so "we already built it" is always true.
- **Only call numbers checked against the do-not-call (DNC) list.** Call between **8am and 9pm in the business's local time**.
- **Text only after a clear verbal yes.** Otherwise send an email.
- **If someone says "don't call me":** apologise, end the call, and add them to the DNC list the same day.
- **Log every call in GoHighLevel (GHL) the same day,** with yourself as Setter Assigned.

## Never use these tactics
- "I'm returning his call," "he's expecting me," "we spoke last week"
- "It's personal," "it's about his account," "it's urgent"
- "I'm calling about your Google listing" (it sounds like we're Google)
- Pretending to be a customer, supplier or friend
- Giving only your first name and refusing to say the company
- Sending the demo link to the gatekeeper to pass along
- Fake scarcity or discounts, or any price other than the approved ballpark
- Promising rankings or results

## Email outreach
- Use your real sender name and company name.
- Include a postal address and a working way to opt out.
- Stop all follow-ups as soon as they reply or opt out.

## Owner to-do (check with a professional)
- **Do-not-call and calling rules:** confirm the federal and state DNC and calling-hours rules for every state you call into. Some states have stricter hours or require registration.
- **Texting:** confirm A2P 10DLC registration and TCPA consent rules for business texting.
- **Call recording:** confirm whether you need one-party or all-party consent in each state you call.
- **Pay and contracts:** confirm whether reps are employees or contractors, minimum wage, and the written commission agreement.
