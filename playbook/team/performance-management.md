# Performance management

## Career ladder

Based on Hormozi's workshop ladder (as summarized in prayceo/hormozi-brain), adapted for a setter → closer team. **Everyone starts at the bottom**, including strong hires from other companies, and no one skips a rung.

| Tier | Role | Promotion criteria ([EXAMPLE], set your own) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Setter (ramping) | Passes the 14-day certification |
| 2 | Setter (certified) | Hits target qualified shows for 4 straight weeks, clean compliance, coachable in tape reviews |
| 3 | Senior setter / setter lead | Top-quartile shows **and** qualification rate for a quarter; helps run pair drills |
| 4 | Closer | Passes closer certification (mock demos scored ≥ 11/14 on the CLOSER scorecard); starts on half a demo calendar |
| 5 | Senior closer | Consistent close rate and cash collected at target, low refunds |
| 6 | Sales manager | Can run role-play and game tape, enforces standards, wants to lead |

The workshop reports Acquisition.com reps reaching #1 in about 3 months on average; treat that as their result, not a promise.

## Calendar gating

- New setters dial in limited blocks; new closers get half a demo calendar.
- A **full calendar is earned by hitting KPIs**, not by tenure.
- The calendar can be reduced when performance drops for 2+ weeks, as a coaching step, not a punishment. Explain why and what will restore it.

## Weekly 1:1 (20–30 min)

```
1. Numbers (5 min): rep reads their own funnel for the week. Where did they lose the most?
2. Tape (10 min): one call the rep picks + one the manager picks. Find the moment it turned.
3. Drill (5 min): role-play that moment, interrupt-and-redo.
4. Commitments (5 min): one skill goal, one activity goal. Write them in GHL notes.
5. Anything blocking you? (list, schedule, tools, personal)
```

Rule: the rep talks first about their numbers. The goal is a rep who can diagnose themselves.

## Skill vs. will

| | Has the skill | Lacks the skill |
|---|---|---|
| **High effort** | Promote / give a bigger calendar | Train harder: more role-play, targeted tape |
| **Low effort** | Direct conversation about expectations; time-boxed | Usually exit |

Hormozi's preference (per the workshop): a 3/10 who hustles over a 6/10 who clocks in and out, because the hustler improves fast.

## Culling: what Hormozi calls "cancers"

- **Obvious:** lying to prospects, falsifying CRM data, harassing teammates, ignoring compliance. Remove promptly (follow your HR process and local law).
- **The dangerous one:** the "5/10 who doesn't try to win." They look acceptable, so they're tolerated, and the team's standard drifts down to them.
- **Culture audit:** "What am I tolerating, allowing or praising?" Unspoken rules ("top closers can skip the script", "no one checks the EOD") drive behavior more than pay.
- Hormozi says a leader may need to cut half of a mediocre team to build a great one. That's his view of a turnaround, not a default action. Train first, then decide on evidence.

## Performance improvement plan (PIP) template

Use only after coaching has been tried and documented.

```
Rep: ______   Role: ______   Manager: ______   Start: ____  Review: ____ (2–4 weeks)

1. The gap (specific, measured):
   e.g. Qualified shows averaged 4/week over the last 4 weeks vs. target 10/week.
2. What the tape shows:
   e.g. Openers run 25+ seconds; no booking ask in 6 of 8 owner conversations reviewed.
3. Support provided:
   e.g. Daily 20-min role-play on opener + booking ask; 2 tape reviews/week with manager.
4. Success criteria:
   e.g. ≥8 qualified shows/week for the final 2 weeks; booking ask on 90%+ of owner conversations reviewed.
5. Check-ins: [dates]
6. Outcome if criteria not met: [role change / end of employment, per company policy]
Signatures
```

Keep it factual and fair. Check employment law before ending anyone's employment; this skill isn't legal advice.

## Exits

- Be direct and kind, and do it quickly once decided.
- Collect CRM access and any customer data the same day.
- Do a short post-mortem: was this a hiring miss (screen better) or a training miss (fix onboarding)?
