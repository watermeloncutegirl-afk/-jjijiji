# Vanguard Setter Cheat Sheet: Modules 01–03 on one page

The full modules are in `vanguard-gatekeeper`, `vanguard-cold-call-opener` and `vanguard-price-question`. Use these lines word for word, and fill in `[brackets]` only with things that are true.

## 01 · Gatekeeper

**Who answers:** rarely a trained receptionist.
- Salon or barber: front desk, or a stylist between clients.
- Trades: the owner's spouse or the office manager. Treat them as an influencer.
- Auto or towing: a service writer or dispatcher.
- Restaurant: a host or a manager in the middle of service.

**Mindset:** ask for help, not access.
- Sound like you belong: talk slower than feels natural and let statements go down at the end. Say one or two sentences, then stop.
- Answer *who* and *why* in one breath.
- When they push back, use the ledge first: "Totally fair."
- Get their name: "Thanks, Jess. I'll try him at 7:30."

**Openers**
- Owner's name known: "Hi, this is Jordan with Vanguard. Is Mike around?"
- Owner's name unknown: "Hi, I'm hoping you can help me out. Jordan with Vanguard. Who's the owner there?"
- Suspicious answerer: "I'll be straight with you, this is a sales call. We already built a demo website for [Business], and I'm trying to get 15 minutes with the owner to show it to him. Is Mike in today?"
- Busy counter: "Hey, sounds busy over there, I'll be quick. Is Tasha free between clients at all today?"

**Smokescreens** (ledge, then one honest sentence, then a small ask; after 2 pushbacks, get a time or leave a message)

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| What's this regarding? | "Good question. We built a free demo website for [Business], and I wanted to show it to Mike. Is he in?" |
| Is he expecting your call? | "No, he's not. This is a cold call, I'll be honest. Is there a better time to catch him?" |
| He's busy / with a client / on a job. | "No problem, I figured. Is he easier to catch between clients, or after he's off the job site?" |
| Send us an email. | "Happy to. What's Mike's best email? And when should I follow up by phone after he's had a look?" |
| We're not interested. | "Totally fair. Is website stuff Mike's call? When's a good time for two minutes? If he says no, I'll leave you alone." |
| We already have someone. | "Oh nice, who handles it? We built a version that fixes [the gap]. Mike can compare in 15 minutes." |
| Can I take a message? | "Yes, please. Jordan from Vanguard, we built a free demo website for [Business], my number's [number]. When should I try him back?" |
| Who are you with? | "Vanguard. We're a small web design company for local businesses. Is Mike around?" |
| He doesn't take sales calls. | "That's fair, I'd probably screen this too. It's already built, though. How does he like to see stuff like this?" |
| Just tell me and I'll pass it on. | "I appreciate that. I'd rather walk him through it himself so he gets the full picture. When's a good time to reach him?" |

**Timing** (their local time, and only between 8am and 9pm)

| Niche | When to call |
|---|---|
| Salon / barber | Just after opening, or 2–4pm Tue–Thu. Never Saturday. |
| Trades / cleaners | First thing in the morning, lunch, or after 5. |
| Restaurants | 2–4pm. Never during lunch or dinner service. |
| Gyms / martial arts | 10am–2pm. Never 5–8am or the 4–8pm class blocks. |
| Auto repair | At opening, the mid-afternoon lull, or near close. |
| Daycare | Early-afternoon nap time. |

**Leave every call with three things:** the owner's name, the gatekeeper's name, and a specific callback time. Then call at exactly that time.

## 02 · Opener & rapport

**Goal:** the first 60 seconds earn the next 60.

**Gong data** (mostly B2B, so treat it as direction): a permission-based opener booked 11.18%, "How's your day going?" 7.6%, and "Did I catch you at a bad time?" 2.15%. Stating a clear reason for the call doubles success.

**Delivery:**
- Slow and low.
- Under 10 seconds.
- Say it in your own words.
- Smile and stand up.

**Openers**
- **Default:** "[Name], this is Jordan with Vanguard. I'll be honest, this is a cold call. Can I take 30 seconds to tell you why I called, and then you tell me if it's worth keeping going?"
  - Then: "I found [Business] on Google, [true detail], but there's no website, just the listing. So we built you a demo. It's done. I'd love to show it to you."
- **Reviews:** "The reason I'm calling: I was reading your Google reviews. People keep mentioning [real theme]. But when someone searches you, there's no website to land on. So we built one."
- **Guarded owner:** "You probably get a lot of calls from website people, so I'll keep this short and different. We didn't call to pitch you. We already built a demo site for [Business]. All I'd like is 15 minutes to show it to you. Fair?"
- **On the go:** "I know I'm probably catching you on a job, so I'll be quick. We built a free demo website for [Business]. Can I grab 15 minutes, early morning or after you're off site?"

**Rapport**
- One specific, true detail.
- Mirror their last 1–3 words as a question, then go quiet.
- Label what you hear ("Sounds like you're slammed."), then pause.
- Match their energy.
- Use their name at the start and once before the booking ask. Use the title they go by: Coach, Doc, Chef.

**Call flow** (2–5 minutes)
1. Opener, under 10 seconds.
2. Reason plus the demo hook: "It's done."
3. Two or three light questions, then mirror and label.
4. Offer the demo, tied to what they said: "15 minutes with the person who'd build it. No obligation."
5. Ask for the booking with two options: "Tuesday or Wednesday?" Then stop talking.
6. Send the link now. Text only after a clear yes; otherwise email. "Did that come through?"

**Quick saves**

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| Who is this? | "It's Jordan with Vanguard. We build websites for local businesses, and we already built a demo site for [Business]." |
| How did you get my number? | "Fair question. Your business number is listed on [Google]. I was looking up [niche] in [city] and came across you." |
| Is this a scam? Are you Google? | "No, and I'm glad you asked. We're not Google. I'm happy to send the demo link so you can look before you agree to anything." |
| Bad time. | "No problem at all. 20 minutes from now, or after 5?" Then call at exactly that time. |

## 03 · "What does it cost?"

**The rule:** if they had to ask twice, they get the number. Never make them ask a third time.

**Four steps**
1. Acknowledge: "Fair question, and I'll give you a straight answer."
2. Say it depends, and ask **one** fit question: "…are people mostly calling you right now, or booking online?"
3. On the second ask, give the ballpark and pause: "Most of our builds land around two thousand, one-time, and the demo itself is free."
4. Bridge: "The exact number depends on what you need, and that's what [Closer] works out when he shows you the demo. 15 minutes. Is Tuesday or Thursday better?"

**Approved numbers only**

| Topic | What to say |
|---|---|
| Typical build | "Most of our builds land around two thousand, one-time." |
| Booking + gallery | "Around twenty-six hundred." |
| Monthly | "The build is one-time. There's an optional care plan, about two hundred a month, cancel anytime." |
| Demo | "Free. No card, no contract." |
| Timeline | "Live 14 days after your kick-off call." Never "14 days from today." |
| Discounts or terms | "I can't set prices or terms. That's [Closer]'s call, and I won't guess." |

**Follow-ups**

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| Too expensive. | "Yeah, it's not a small number. What were you expecting?" … "Worst case, you see a free demo and decide it's not for you." |
| Wix is $20. | "You can, and for some businesses that's right. The difference is who does the work. If both cost the same, would you rather build it yourself or have it done?" |
| My nephew. | "That's great. Is he building it now, or is it more of a 'one day' thing?" |
| Just email the price. | "I'll save you the wait: most builds land around two thousand, one-time. I'll email the demo now. What's the best address?" |
| What's the catch? | "Really free. The honest catch: we hope you like it enough to have us make it your real site. If not, you've lost 15 minutes." |

**Fit questions** (ask 2 or 3)
- Are they the owner or decision-maker?
- Are they taking on new customers?
- How do most new customers find them?
- "If it was right, is around two thousand in the range you'd consider?" If they say "$300 max", don't book; offer to email the demo.

**Up-front contract:** "So here's how the call works. 15 to 30 minutes with [Closer]. He shows you the demo, asks a few questions, and gives you an exact price. If it's not for you, just say so. Fair?"
