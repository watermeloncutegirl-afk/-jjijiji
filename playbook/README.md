# Vanguard Studios Agency Playbook

Everything about the offer, the sales process, the team and delivery, in one place. Items in [brackets] are things for you to fill in. Start with `12-open-decisions.md`.

| # | Document | For |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | [Agency and offer overview](01-agency-and-offer.md) | Everyone: what we sell, who to, approved prices, what we never claim |
| 02 | [Offer analysis and upgrade ideas](02-offer-analysis.md) | Owner: value equation audit, suggested guarantee, bonuses, tiers |
| 03 | [Sales process, start to finish](03-sales-process.md) | Everyone: pipeline stages, roles, handoff note, KPIs |
| 04 | [Leads, prospect audit and demo brief](04-leads-audit-demo.md) | Research and design: building lists, auditing prospects, briefing the demo |
| 05 | [Setter scripts cheat sheet](05-setter-scripts.md) | Setters: gatekeeper, opener and price, on one page |
| 06 | [Outreach sequence](06-outreach-sequence.md) | Setters: 14-day cadence, voicemails, compliant cold emails |
| 07 | [Closer playbook](07-closer-playbook.md) | Closer: demo call agenda, proposal one-pager, CLOSER framework, objections |
| 08 | [Client onboarding and delivery](08-client-onboarding-delivery.md) | Delivery: kick-off, 14-day timeline, launch checklist |
| 09 | [Job post: appointment setter](09-job-post-setter.md) | Hiring |
| 10 | [Compliance and ground rules](10-compliance-and-ground-rules.md) | Everyone: these are non-negotiable |
| 12 | [Open decisions](12-open-decisions.md) | Owner: what you still need to decide or supply |

**Team management** (`team/`): hiring, compensation, rep onboarding and training, KPIs and diagnostics, performance management, daily and weekly cadence.

**Setter training** (`training/`): drills, role-play guide, the 100-point call scorecard, the 14-day setter onboarding plan.

**Related:** the Claude skills in `.claude/skills/` run these documents interactively, through role-plays, call reviews and drafted documents. Research on other sales skills is in `SALES_SKILLS_RESEARCH.md`; setup instructions are in `HOW_TO_USE.md`.

> Dollar figures for pay and AI pricing are examples. The only approved customer-facing prices are those in `01-agency-and-offer.md`.
