# Job Post: Appointment Setter (Cold Calling)

**Vanguard Studios · [Remote / City, State] · [Full-time / Part-time] · [Hours, e.g. Mon–Fri, 9am–5pm in our customers' time zones]**

## About us
Vanguard Studios builds websites for local businesses such as salons, barbers, roofers, auto shops, restaurants and gyms. Before we ever call a business, we build its demo website for free. So when you call, you aren't asking an owner to buy something they can't picture. You're offering to show them a website that's already been built for their business.

## The role
You call local business owners, get past whoever answers, and book a 15-minute demo call with our closer. You don't sell, quote custom prices or negotiate. Your job is to start honest conversations that turn into booked demos.

**A typical day:**
- A morning huddle and a short role-play with the team
- Call blocks timed to when each type of business picks up (salons after opening, trades first thing in the morning, restaurants between 2pm and 4pm)
- Getting past the front desk honestly, opening the call and booking the demo
- Logging every call in our CRM (GoHighLevel) the same day
- An end-of-day report, plus a weekly call review where we go through your recordings with you

## What you'll get
- **Pay:** $[__]/hour base, plus $[__] for each qualified demo that shows up, plus $[__] for each deal that closes and is paid. Realistic total: **$[__]–$[__] per month** at full ramp.
- **Paid training before you dial.** You get 2 weeks of scripts, drills and role-play, and you go live once you pass certification.
- **Coaching every day.** No one gets left to figure it out alone.
- **A clear path to closer** when you hit [criteria].
- **A product that's easy to talk about.** "We already built it" is a much easier first sentence than a pitch.

## You are
- **Comfortable talking to strangers.** Phones, retail, hospitality, call centres, door-to-door or serving tables all count as experience. Sales experience is welcome but not required.
- **Coachable.** When you get feedback in the morning, you use it on your calls that afternoon.
- **Fine hearing "no" a lot.** Most calls don't book, and that doesn't throw you.
- **Fast and reliable.** You call back at exactly the time you said you would.
- **Honest.** We never fake familiarity, urgency, referrals or callbacks.

## How we work
- We only call numbers that have been checked against the do-not-call list, and only between 8am and 9pm in the business's own time zone.
- If someone says "don't call me", we apologise and remove them the same day.
- We text a business only after they've clearly said yes on the phone.
