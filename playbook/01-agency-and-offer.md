# Vanguard Studios: Agency and Offer Overview

## What we do
Vanguard Studios builds websites for local businesses that don't have one. These businesses rely on a Google listing or Facebook page, or have a site that's broken or doesn't work on phones.

**What makes us different:** we build a demo website for the business *before* we ever call. When we call, we aren't pitching an idea. We say: "We already built it. It's done. Can I show you?"

## Who we sell to (ideal customer)
| Niche | Why they fit | Who answers the phone |
|---|---|---|
| Salons, barbers, groomers | Customers search and book on their phones; reviews matter | Front desk, or a stylist between clients |
| Trades (roofers, plumbers, HVAC, cleaners) | High job value; customers Google them before calling | Spouse or office manager; the owner is on a job |
| Auto repair and towing | Searched in urgent moments | Service writer or dispatcher |
| Restaurants | Menus, hours and photos are checked before visiting | Host or manager |
| Gyms and martial arts | Schedules, trial classes, booking | Front desk or a coach |
| Daycares | Parents research heavily before calling | Director |

**Signs a business is a good fit:**
- No website, only a Google listing
- A Facebook-only presence
- A broken site, or one that doesn't work on phones
- Strong reviews
- Taking on new customers
- The owner can be reached

**Signs to skip a business:**
- It's booked solid and not taking new customers
- The owner says the most they'd pay is around $300
- It's a franchise with a corporate site
- The number is on the do-not-call (DNC) list

## The offer (approved terms)
| Item | Approved wording |
|---|---|
| Demo | Free. No card, no contract. Built before the first call. |
| Typical build | "Most of our builds land around two thousand, one-time." |
| Bigger build (booking and gallery) | "Around twenty-six hundred." |
| Care plan (optional) | "About two hundred a month, cancel anytime." |
| Timeline | "Live 14 days after your kick-off call." Never "14 days from today." |
| Exact price, discounts, payment plans, terms | Set only by the closer on the demo call |

## What's included in a build
- A site of about 5 pages, built from the demo they've already seen (services, about, reviews, contact, plus booking and gallery on the bigger build)
- Tap-to-call and a booking or quote button on every page
- Their services, hours, photos and real reviews
- Designed for phones first, connected to their domain, and linked from their Google Business Profile
- Basic on-page SEO. We never promise rankings.

**Care plan:** hosting, updates and small edits. It's optional and can be cancelled anytime.

## Value proposition (how to talk about it)
- **Outcome, not features:** "When people Google you, they find you, trust you and call you."
- **Proof:** "It's already built. You've seen it."
- **Speed:** "Live 14 days after kick-off."
- **No effort for them:** "We build it, from your reviews and listing. You approve it."

## What we never claim
- Rankings ("you'll be #1 on Google")
- Results ("20 more customers a month")
- Fake urgency, scarcity or discounts
- Anything negative about Wix, freelancers, Facebook or a nephew who builds sites
