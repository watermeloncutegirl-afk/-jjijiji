# Unified 100-Point Call Scorecard

Score only what the transcript shows. If a stage never happened (for example, the owner answered directly), mark it **N/A** and rescale: `score / points available × 100`.

## Hard fails (an automatic FAIL, whatever the points)

A call fails if the setter did any of the following.

**Honesty and identity**
- Didn't say their name and "Vanguard" in the first sentence to whoever answered.
- Gave only a first name and refused to say the company.
- Claimed a relationship: "I'm returning his call," "he's expecting me," or "we spoke last week."
- Used a false pretext: "It's personal," "it's about his account," or "it's urgent."
- Said "I'm calling about your Google listing," or implied they were Google.
- Pretended to be a customer, supplier or friend to get the owner or his cell number.
- Mentioned a made-up referral, or used fake familiarity ("How have you been?" to a stranger).
- Said "We already built it" when no demo existed, or used an unverified detail as fact (for example, "love your 5-star reviews" when the business has 3.8).

**Price and promises**
- Gave any number other than the approved ones, such as "a few hundred" or "like fifteen hundred."
- Said "It's cheap" or "it's basically nothing."
- Offered a discount, a "special this month," or "only two spots left," or set any terms.
- Promised results: "You'll rank #1" or "you'll get 20 more customers."

**Compliance**
- Texted without a clear verbal yes.
- Ignored or argued with "Don't call me."
- Called outside 8am–9pm their time, if the transcript shows the time.
- Sent the demo link to the gatekeeper to "pass along."

## Sections

### 1. Gatekeeper (Module 01): 20 points
| Points | Criterion |
|---|---|
| 4 | Answered who and why in one breath, and sounded like they belonged: short, then stopped |
| 4 | Used the right opener for the situation: owner's name known, help ask, straight-shooter, or busy counter |
| 5 | Handled smokescreens with the ledge, then one honest sentence, then a small ask |
| 3 | Obeyed the two-pushback rule, then got a time or left a message |
| 4 | Left with the owner's name, the gatekeeper's name and a specific callback time |

### 2. Opener & rapport (Module 02): 25 points
| Points | Criterion |
|---|---|
| 6 | Opener under about 10 seconds (25 words or fewer): name, Vanguard, honest framing, permission ask, then stopped |
| 5 | Gave a clear reason for the call, plus the demo hook ("It's done") |
| 5 | Used one specific, verified detail, not generic flattery |
| 5 | Used rapport tools: mirrored, labeled, matched energy, used their name without overdoing it |
| 4 | Didn't pitch features in the first 15 seconds, didn't back off at "Is now a good time?", didn't talk over them |

### 3. Discovery & fit: 15 points
| Points | Criterion |
|---|---|
| 5 | Asked 2 or 3 light questions, not seven in a row |
| 5 | Confirmed the decision-maker and asked at least one more fit question (taking new customers, how they find customers, budget range) |
| 5 | Listened and logged usable notes for the Closer, and didn't book a clear non-fit (for example, "$300 max") |

### 4. Price handling (Module 03): 20 points
Mark this N/A if price never came up.
| Points | Criterion |
|---|---|
| 4 | Acknowledged the question honestly, with no dodge like "Great question!" |
| 4 | Said it depends and asked **one** real fit question, at most once |
| 6 | Gave the approved ballpark by the second ask, then paused |
| 4 | Bridged to the Closer and a two-option booking ask |
| 2 | Handled any follow-up with an approved line and didn't trash Wix, freelancers or the nephew |

### 5. Booking & close-out: 15 points
| Points | Criterion |
|---|---|
| 4 | Tied the demo offer to what the prospect said |
| 4 | Asked with two options ("Tuesday or Wednesday?"), then stopped talking |
| 4 | Set up the up-front contract: 15–30 minutes, the Closer shows the demo, gives an exact price, and "no" is okay |
| 3 | Sent the link right away (text only after a yes, otherwise email) and confirmed "Did that come through?" |

### 6. Delivery: 5 points
| Points | Criterion |
|---|---|
| 2 | Pace and tone: short sentences, statements ending down, calm |
| 2 | Talk ratio: let the prospect talk and used silence after asks |
| 1 | Sounded natural rather than read |

## Grading bands
| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 90–100 | Certified level: use the call as a training example |
| 75–89 | Solid: fix the single priority item |
| 60–74 | Developing: daily drills on the weakest section |
| Under 60 | Back to drills before more live dials on this skill |

## Finding "the second it turned"
Find the exact line after which the prospect's engagement changed. Signs include:
- shorter or longer replies
- a question back to the setter
- a sigh, or "I gotta go"
- a tone shift to suspicion

Quote the setter line just before it. Weekly reviews use three calls (one booked, one hang-up, one "send me info") and name the pattern across them.
