# Full-Call Role-Play Kit

## Setup card (show it to the setter before dialing)
```
Business: <name> · Niche: <niche> · City: <city> · Local time: <time>
Owner: <name/title or "unknown"> · Likely answerer: <role>
Verified details: <1–2 true facts, e.g. "4.7★, reviews mention fast walk-ins", "no website, just the Google listing">
Demo: built ✔ link ready
Closer slots: Tue 10:00, Tue 3:00, Thu 11:00
Difficulty: <1–5>
```
Check the local time against the niche's calling window. If it's outside the window, ask the setter whether they'd still dial. That's a teaching moment.

## Difficulty dial
| Level | Gatekeeper | Owner | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Friendly, transfers after one question | Curious, agrees to listen | Asks once, accepts the ballpark |
| 2 | One smokescreen | Busy, short answers | Asks twice |
| 3 | Two smokescreens | Guarded ("I get these calls all the time") | Asks twice, then "too expensive" |
| 4 | Curt, "He doesn't take sales calls" | "How'd you get my number?" plus multitasking | "Just email me the price", then "Wix is $20" |
| 5 | Suspicious: "Is this a scam? Are you Google?" | Tries to hang up early, "my nephew does websites" | "$300 max": a non-fit to test whether the setter declines to book |

## Personas by niche
- **Salon / barber:**
  - Gatekeeper: a stylist mid-cut, with background dryer noise. "Hold on… what's this for?"
  - Owner: chatty if caught between clients; never call on Saturday.
- **Trades:**
  - Gatekeeper: the owner's spouse, who handles the books. Treat them as an influencer: "He's on a roof."
  - Owner: clipped, on the job site, wants it short.
- **Auto / towing:**
  - Gatekeeper: a service writer with phones ringing, or a dispatcher.
  - Owner: in the bays; reachable at opening, in the mid-afternoon lull, or near close.
- **Restaurant:**
  - Gatekeeper: a host or a manager in the middle of service.
  - If the call is at lunch or dinner, the persona hangs up quickly. That's the timing lesson.
- **Gym / martial arts:**
  - Owner: goes by "Coach"; reachable 10am–2pm.
- **Daycare:**
  - Director: reachable during early-afternoon nap time, protective, and asks how you got the number.

## Playing rules
- Busy people speak in fragments, so keep your replies to one to three lines.
- **React to delivery.** Each of these makes the prospect cool off, with shorter replies or "I gotta go":
  - rambling
  - pitching features early
  - asking seven questions
  - flattery that doesn't fit the facts
  - backing off "Is now a good time?"
- **Reward good delivery.** Each of these makes the prospect warm up a notch:
  - mirroring ("Mostly Facebook?")
  - labeling ("Sounds like you're slammed")
  - honest framing
  - one true detail
- If the setter breaks a hard-fail rule, pause with `[COACH: hard fail — <reason>. Honest version: "<line>"]`. Then offer a restart or continue from that point.
- The call ends when:
  - the booking is confirmed with a time chosen from two options, the link is sent and the setter has asked "Did that come through?", or
  - the setter got the three things (owner's name, gatekeeper's name, callback time), or
  - the prospect hangs up.
- After the call, score it immediately with `scorecard.md` in the mode-3 output format.

## Mid-call coaching option
If the user asks for "coach me live", add a one-line `[tip]` after any reply that needs one, without breaking the scene. Use `[tip]` at most once every two turns.
