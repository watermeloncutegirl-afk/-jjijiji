# 14-Day New-Setter Onboarding

The approach is Hormozi-style: **memorise, drill, shadow, certify, then dial live.** Practice is 30 minutes a day for the first two weeks, on top of the day's other work. Reps beat reading: setters learn the script word for word, then say it in their own words. As Hormozi puts it, "A script that sounds like a robot loses."

Setters don't make live dials until they pass the Day 10 certification.

## Week 1: Learn the words

| Day | Focus | Daily practice (30 min) | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ground rules, the offer, the demo, compliance, logging in GHL | Read all three modules aloud once. Run the opener out loud 10 times, recorded. | Recites the non-negotiables from memory |
| 2 | Module 01: who answers, mindset, the 4 gatekeeper openers | Opener ×10. Gatekeeper openers ×5 each. | Delivers all 4 openers word for word |
| 3 | Module 01: the 10 smokescreens, the two-pushback rule, timing | Smokescreen rapid-fire ×10 in random order | 8/10 with ledge, honest sentence, small ask |
| 4 | Module 02: the Gong data, delivery, the 4 owner openers | Opener ×10, each 25 words or fewer | 9/10 under 10 seconds |
| 5 | Module 02: rapport (mirror, label, match energy), the 6-step call flow, quick saves | Rapport drill: coach plays the owner, setter mirrors and labels 5 times | Uses mirror and label naturally |
| 6 | Module 03: the rule, why they ask, the 4 steps, approved numbers | Two-ask price drill ×5 | Gives the ballpark on the 2nd ask every time, with no wrong numbers |
| 7 | Module 03: follow-ups, fit questions, up-front contract. Review the week. | Price follow-ups ×5, plus a written quiz on numbers and off-limits lines | 100% on the numbers and off-limits quiz |

## Week 2: Make it sound human, then go live

| Day | Focus | Daily practice (30 min) | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | Full role-plays at difficulty 1–2, across 3 niches | 3 full calls, each scored | Average 70+, no hard fails |
| 9 | Shadow: listen to 10 or more live calls from a top setter, log what you hear, and name the second each call turned | Smokescreens plus price drill ×5 | Shadow notes reviewed |
| 10 | **Certification** | 3 role-plays at difficulty 3, plus random smokescreens and the price drill | **80+ on every call, zero hard fails, recites every approved price** |
| 11 | First live block: 1–2 hours, with a manager listening in | Opener ×10 before the block | Logs every call in GHL the same day |
| 12 | Live block plus a same-day review of 3 calls | Drill the weakest section from yesterday | Fixes yesterday's priority item |
| 13 | Live block with a full calendar, and pre-shift and pre-dial checklists done without prompting | Difficulty 4 role-play | Checklists 100% |
| 14 | Weekly call review (one booked, one hang-up, one "send me info") and a plan for week 3 | Review session | Signed off to dial solo |

## Ongoing (after Day 14)
- **Daily:** a 5-minute pre-shift warm-up (opener ×3, 3 random smokescreens, 1 price round).
- **Weekly:** a game-tape review of 3 calls; drill the weakest phase for the week.
- **Watch the metrics:**
  - dials
  - conversations
  - demos booked
  - **show rate**
  - demos the Closer rates as qualified
  - Booking a non-fit is not a win.
- **Re-certify** anyone who scores a hard fail on a live call before they dial again.

## Coach notes
- Interrupt early and often in drills: "Stop. Go back. Try it like this." Then model the line.
- Rotate the focus of each session: gatekeeper, opener, discovery, price, booking.
- Coachability and speed of improvement predict success better than early polish.
