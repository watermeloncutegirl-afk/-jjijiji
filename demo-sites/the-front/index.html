Eating here

No kitchen. Bring your own.

It is the question the bar gets asked most, so it gets its own answer here instead of being buried. There is no food served at The Front, and you are welcome to bring your own in and eat it with a pint.

  1. 1Pick up fish and chips, a pasty or whatever you fancy from the quay.
  2. 2Bring it in and find a table, inside under the vaults or out on the quay.
  3. 3Order at the bar. Nine handpumps, plus cider and the usual.

For the owner: reviews also mention a seafood van outside. If that is a regular fixture, it is worth naming here, with its days.

Groups and private hire

Coming in with a crowd

There is no phone number published for The Front anywhere online, so right now a group of twelve has no way to give the bar any warning. This form is the fix, and it works while the bar is shut.

Why not a table booking tool

A quayside bar fills on walk-ins and should keep doing that. The only booking worth taking online is the big group or the private do, where somebody needs an answer in writing.

This is a demo, so nothing was sent. On the live site your enquiry goes straight to The Front by email, and you get a copy of what you sent. The bar would come back to you about the date and the space.
