Eating here

No kitchen. Bring your own.

It's the thing we get asked most, so it gets a straight answer rather than being buried. We don't do food. You're very welcome to bring your own in and eat it with a pint.

  1. 1Grab fish and chips, a pasty, whatever you fancy, from the quay.
  2. 2Bring it in and find a table, inside under the vaults or out on the quay.
  3. 3Order at the bar. Nine handpumps, plus cider and the usual.

Note for the owner: reviews also mention a seafood van outside. If that's a regular fixture, it's worth naming here with its days.

Groups and private hire

Coming in with a crowd

There's no phone number for us published anywhere online, so a group of twelve has no way of giving us any warning. This form fixes that, and it works while we're shut.

Why not a table booking tool

We fill on people walking in off the quay and we'd like to keep it that way. The only booking worth taking online is the big group or the private do, where somebody needs an answer in writing.

This is a demo, so nothing was sent. On the live site your enquiry comes straight to us by email and you get a copy of what you sent. We'd come back to you about the date and the space.
