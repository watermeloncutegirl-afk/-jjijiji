Private hire

Have the café to yourselves, after hours

We've had birthdays, Christmas get togethers and a 95th down here of an evening. At the moment every one of those starts with a phone call, so here is a form you can send us while we're shut.

This is a demo, so nothing was sent. On the live site your enquiry comes straight to us by email and you get a copy of what you sent. We'd come back to you about dates, numbers and what we can do on food.

Why a form and not a booking system

We don't need a seat booking tool for a coffee and a toastie. Daytime, you just walk in. The one thing worth catching online is the evening do, where somebody is weighing us up against another place and wants an answer in writing.

Note for the owner: capacity, minimum numbers, which evenings you take and what food you offer are all still to confirm, so none of them appear on this page.
