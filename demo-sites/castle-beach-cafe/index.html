The café, to yourselves, after hours

Reviews mention evening parties at the café, including a 95th birthday and Christmas get togethers. At the moment every one of those starts with a phone call. This form means an enquiry can land while the café is shut.

This is a demo, so nothing was sent. On the live site your enquiry goes straight to Castle Beach Café by email, and you get a copy of what you sent. The café would reply with dates, numbers and what they can do on food.

Why this, and not a booking system

A beach café does not need a seat booking tool for a coffee and a toastie. Daytime trade walks in. The thing worth catching online is the evening booking, where someone is deciding between venues and wants an answer in writing.

For the owner: capacity, minimum numbers, which evenings you take and what food you offer are all still to confirm, so none of them appear on this page.
