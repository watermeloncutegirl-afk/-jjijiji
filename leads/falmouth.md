## Methodology

1. **Candidate lists.**
   - OpenStreetMap via the Overpass API (the overpass-api.de mirror was unreachable; overpass.kumi.systems and maps.mail.ru worked). Query: shop/amenity/craft/office/tourism/fitness in bbox 50.135,-5.105 to 50.165,-5.055. That gave about 395 named POIs with no `website`, `contact:website`, `url` or brand tag. Penryn (TR10, lon < about -5.09 / lat > about 50.165) was dropped.
   - The Falmouth BID / town directory (falmouth.co.uk "explore" pages): all 310 business pages were scraped. The ~55 with no "Read More" website button, or only Facebook/Instagram links, were kept.
   - Targeted web searches for trades and services (electricians, dog groomers, garages, phone repair).
2. **Verification.** Each candidate got an extended web search for "<name> Falmouth" (plus street). The results were checked for an own-domain site. Anything with a real site was **excluded**, e.g. Balti Curries, Ming's Garden, Gurkha, Asha, Max Bagels, Fal Falafel, Marmaris, Kebab on the Corner, Curry Corner, Clay's, Mia's, Tailored Cuts, Waves, NV, Ava Nails, Beauty Loft, Toast, Bean, Time and Tide, Admiral Lounge, Xpress Garage, Smart Electrical and Kernow Klips. Myatts butcher (closed Sept 2025) was also excluded.
3. **Counts as "no website":** Facebook/Instagram only, booking-platform pages (Booksy, Fresha, Setmore, SumUp), directory listings (Yell, Cylex, TripAdvisor, falmouth.co.uk), and free subdomains (Wix `*.wixsite.com`, Google Sites). Those last two are flagged Medium as "upgrade" prospects.
4. **Confidence.**
   - High: searched, and no own domain appeared.
   - Medium: some ambiguity, e.g. a free subdomain, an unconfirmed domain in one directory, a status or address conflict, or a summary hinting at a site.
5. **Contact details** are only those publicly listed for the business. Where sources conflict, both numbers are shown. Sole-trader home addresses are given to street level only.

**Caveats:** Web search can miss a newly launched site, so do a quick Google check before outreach. Phone numbers and opening status come from third-party directories and may be stale.
